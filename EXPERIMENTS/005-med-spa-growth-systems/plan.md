# Med Spa Growth Systems Plan

## Experiment Question

Will med spas pay for one focused growth improvement selected from their visible consultation, treatment, package, reputation, or follow-up journey?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Med spa customer journey audit + one focused implementation module
First modules to test: treatment consultation flow, Botox/filler campaign page, consultation follow-up, lapsed-client reactivation
Target market: Med spas and aesthetic clinics
First market: Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
Price test: $1,250 setup
Optional add-on: $350/month
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

Med spas often have profitable treatments and repeat-client potential, but public pages may bury consultation CTAs, make packages unclear, underuse reviews, or lack simple follow-up. A focused module can be easier to sell than a full marketing retainer.

## Locked Direction

```text
Niche: Med spas / aesthetics clinics
First target market: Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
Market rollout: Scottsdale/Phoenix -> Boca Raton/Fort Lauderdale/West Palm Beach -> Dallas/Plano/Frisco -> Miami -> Los Angeles/Orange County
Buyer: Owner, clinic manager, medical director, or marketing decision-maker
Initial offer hypothesis: Treatment booking funnel + follow-up system for one high-margin service
Outreach rule: Use a shared med spa outreach frame, but customize the observed issue for each business.
```

## Out Of Scope

- Medical advice or claims.
- HIPAA-sensitive data handling.
- Ad campaign management.
- Guaranteed patients, bookings, outcomes, reviews, revenue, rankings, or ad results.

## Decision Rules

- Continue if Scottsdale / Phoenix produces 1 paid pilot, 2 serious calls, or 3-5 positive replies.
- Revise the offer if owners show interest but prefer a different wedge, such as lapsed-client reactivation, package clarity, reviews, or booking cleanup.
- Move to the next market only after the advancement rule in `roadmap.md` is reached.
- Stop or pause if 20-50 qualified prospects show no credible pain or response.
