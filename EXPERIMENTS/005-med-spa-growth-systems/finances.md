## Margin Notes

Med spas can support recurring support if modules help refresh campaigns, maintain follow-up sequences, and organize approved proof. Compliance and claims risk must stay low.

## Paid Add-On Ideas

- Monthly page updates and campaign refreshes.
- Follow-up workflow maintenance.
- Review and proof placement updates.
- Basic reporting and tracking review.
- Chatbot or AI intake maintenance for approved scripts.
- AI phone follow-up only for narrow use cases such as reminders, reactivation, or missed-call callbacks.
