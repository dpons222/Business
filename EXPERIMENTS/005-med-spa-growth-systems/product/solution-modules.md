# Solution Modules

## 1. Treatment Consultation Flow Cleanup

Clarifies the next step from treatment pages to consultation request or booking.

## 2. Botox / Filler Campaign Page

Creates a focused educational and inquiry path without making outcome claims.

## 3. Laser Hair Removal Or Body Contouring Campaign Page

Frames one treatment category with FAQs, CTA, and tracking plan.

## 4. Package Or Membership Explanation Module

Clarifies package value, who it is for, and how to ask questions.

## 5. Consultation Follow-Up Workflow

Defines response expectations, follow-up copy, status values, and approval process.

## 6. Review And Testimonial Workflow

Organizes approved reputation handling without inventing claims.

## 7. Lapsed-Client Reactivation Campaign

Creates approved email/SMS-style copy for returning clients where appropriate.

## 8. GBP And Booking Link Cleanup

Audits public links and recommends a cleaner path from local search to booking.

## 9. Chatbot Or AI Intake Module

Creates a narrow, approved intake assistant for FAQs, consult routing, or lead capture. This should hand off clearly to a human and avoid treatment advice, diagnosis, clinical claims, and private patient data during validation.

## 10. AI Phone Follow-Up Module

Uses a narrow approved script for reminders, missed-call callbacks, basic reactivation, or qualification. Treat this as an advanced module after trust is built, not the default first offer.
