# Med Spa Growth Systems Validation

## Qualification Criteria

Qualified prospects should have:

- active med spa or aesthetic services,
- location in the current target market,
- public treatment pages,
- visible booking, call, or consultation path,
- one visible gap that can be fixed with a focused module,
- no requirement for private patient data to make the first recommendation.

## Current Target Market

```text
Primary market: Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
Subareas: Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Arcadia / Biltmore Phoenix, Chandler, Gilbert, Tempe
```

## Metrics

Track prospects researched, recommendation category, contact method, outreach sent, replies, objections, calls booked, pilots, and module interest.

## Success Criteria

Continue with 1 paid pilot, 2 serious calls, or 3-5 positive replies. Revise if owners prefer reactivation, treatment packages, reviews, or booking cleanup over the initial wedge. Stop if 20 qualified prospects show no credible pain.

## Market Advancement Rule

Advance, revise, or stop after one of these happens:

- 50 qualified prospects reviewed.
- 20 outreach messages prepared or sent.
- 5 positive replies.
- 2 serious discovery calls booked.
- 1 paid pilot closed.
- The market is marked weak after documented testing.
