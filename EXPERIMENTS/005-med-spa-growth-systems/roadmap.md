# Med Spa Growth Systems Roadmap

## Goal

Validate a profitable, repeatable growth-system offer for med spas and aesthetic clinics, starting with one focused market and expanding only after documented signals.

## Locked Niche Decisions

```text
Niche: Med spas / aesthetics clinics
First target market: Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
Primary buyer: Owner, clinic manager, medical director, or marketing decision-maker
Initial offer hypothesis: Treatment booking funnel + follow-up system for one high-margin service
First validation method: Public customer journey review, personalized recommendation, and direct outreach
```

## Market Rollout Order

1. Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
2. Boca Raton / Fort Lauderdale / West Palm Beach, FL
3. Dallas / Plano / Frisco, TX
4. Miami, FL
5. Los Angeles / Orange County, CA

## Why Start With Scottsdale / Phoenix

Scottsdale is the best first market because it combines affluent local demand, strong aesthetics culture, enough med spa volume, and a smaller outreach environment than Miami, Los Angeles, or New York.

Supporting research:

- The U.S. med spa industry has eclipsed $17B in revenue and is growing by more than $1B annually, according to AmSpa.
- Grand View Research estimates the global medical spa market at $24.2B in 2025 and projects $27.8B in 2026.
- Scottsdale's 2020-2024 median household income is $110,886, according to U.S. Census QuickFacts.
- Arizona, Florida, Texas, California, New York, Georgia, Illinois, and Colorado are among high-volume med spa states in 2026 compliance references.

Sources:

- https://www.americanmedspa.org/med-spa-statistics/
- https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/medical-spa-market
- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/scottsdalecityarizona/PST045225
- https://medspastandards.com/blog/med-spa-regulations-by-state

## Market Advancement Rule

Do not move to the next market just because the list says so. Move only after the current market has enough evidence.

Advance, revise, or stop after one of these happens:

- 50 qualified prospects reviewed.
- 20 outreach messages prepared or sent.
- 5 positive replies.
- 2 serious discovery calls booked.
- 1 paid pilot closed.
- The market is marked weak after documented testing.

## Profitable Customer Actions

Prioritize recommendations that help med spas improve one of these actions:

- Booked consults.
- Online treatment bookings.
- Phone calls.
- Quote or consult requests.
- Membership or package inquiries.
- Lapsed-client reactivation.

## Solution Modules To Test

Start with visible, low-risk modules before advanced automation.

1. Landing page or treatment page improvement.
2. Lead capture and consult request cleanup.
3. Booking CTA cleanup.
4. Review and proof placement.
5. Missed-call or text-back workflow.
6. Email/SMS follow-up workflow.
7. Chatbot or AI intake module.
8. AI phone follow-up for narrow, approved use cases.

## Outreach Framework

Use a shared niche-level frame, but personalize the main observation for each business.

Reusable frame:

```text
I noticed one place your booking flow could make it easier for prospective clients to request a consult for [specific treatment]. I made a short recommendation/demo.
```

Personalization rule:

- Do not send the same observation to every business.
- Base the outreach angle on the prospect's real public site, booking flow, treatment pages, reviews, CTAs, mobile experience, or follow-up gaps.
- Mention one observed opportunity only.
- Avoid medical claims, guaranteed outcomes, and unsupported assumptions.
