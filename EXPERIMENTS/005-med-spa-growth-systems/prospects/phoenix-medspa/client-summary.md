# Phoenix Medspa Client Summary

I noticed one practical opportunity in the public customer journey: Phoenix Medspa already makes pricing, specials, booking, phone/text, and email paths visible, but price-shopping visitors may need a clearer bridge from comparing Botox or filler options to choosing the right next step.

The first improvement I would recommend is a focused Botox/filler pricing-to-consult path. The goal would be to help a visitor understand what to do after seeing pricing or a special offer, then move cleanly toward booking, texting, calling, or asking a question.

This would be a focused first step, not a full site rebuild or a promise of specific results.

## Recommended First Deliverable

- Clarify one Botox/filler next-step path.
- Connect pricing and specials to the booking or text/call action.
- Add first-time visitor context without making treatment claims.
- Keep the recommendation based on public site content only.
