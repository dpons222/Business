# Elixir Medical Spa Client Summary

I noticed one practical opportunity in the public customer journey: Elixir already has strong tox/filler packages and membership options, but a visitor may need help deciding which path fits before they click `Book Now`.

The first improvement I would recommend is a focused package explanation module for one high-value tox/filler package. The goal would be to make the package easier to understand, connect it to the right next step, and clarify whether the visitor should book, complete the self assessment, or ask a question first.

This would be a focused first step, not a full site rebuild or a promise of specific results.

## Recommended First Deliverable

- Pick one package to feature first.
- Clarify who it is for.
- Explain the next step before booking.
- Keep package, membership, and self-assessment paths from competing with each other.
