# Bellagio Med Spa - Arcadia Client Summary

I noticed one practical opportunity in the public customer journey: Bellagio already shows services, structured programs, financing, Arcadia location details, and a consultation path, but first-time visitors may need a clearer explanation of which first step fits them.

The first improvement I would recommend is program and consultation path clarity for the Arcadia location. The goal would be to help visitors connect their treatment interest to a complimentary consultation or structured program without having to interpret every service option at once.

This would be a focused first step, not a full site rebuild or a promise of specific results.

## Recommended First Deliverable

- Clarify the Arcadia first-visit path.
- Tie structured programs to the consultation action.
- Keep financing visible as support, not the main decision.
- Keep the recommendation based on public site content only.
