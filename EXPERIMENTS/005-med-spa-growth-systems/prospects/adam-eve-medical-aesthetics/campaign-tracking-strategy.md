# Adam & Eve Medical Aesthetics Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Primary Recommendation

Treatment consultation flow cleanup

## Signals To Track

- Clicks or taps on the relevant booking, contact, consultation, service, package, or proof path.
- Calls, texts, form starts, bookings, or consultation requests, if available.
- Replies or objections after outreach, if outreach is later approved.

## Notes

Tracking depends on the prospect's tools and access. Do not promise performance outcomes.
