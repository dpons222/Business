# Skin Savvy Aesthetics Recommendation

## Observed Evidence

- The site has visible `BOOK NOW`, Patient Portal, Financing, and phone actions near the top.
- The service menu includes Botox, Dysport, Daxxify, lip fillers, dermal fillers, lasers, collagen stimulators, weight loss, esthetician services, and other treatment categories.
- The homepage positions Skin Savvy as a Scottsdale med spa for injectables, fillers, Botox, facials, laser, and skincare.
- The homepage includes a clear `Schedule a Complimentary Skin Care Consultation` section.
- The homepage also introduces `The Membership` and links to `become a member`.
- The membership page lists Gold at `$79/month` and Diamond at `$149/month`, with treatment-related benefits and a consultation CTA below the membership details.

Sources:

- https://skinsavvyscottsdale.com/
- https://skinsavvyscottsdale.com/membership/

## Assumptions

- Visitors may benefit from a clearer bridge between consultation, membership, financing, and the broad service menu.
- Membership details and pricing should be rechecked before outreach because offers can change.
- Phone and booking paths are public, but the preferred outreach contact method still needs manual verification.

## Primary Recommendation

```text
Membership/package clarity module tied to the complimentary consultation path.
```

## Why This First

Skin Savvy already has a strong menu, consultation CTA, and membership offer. The most practical first improvement is to make the membership path easier to understand in relation to consultation and repeat treatments.

## First Deliverable

A lightweight membership-path recommendation brief showing:

- where membership fits in the first-time consultation journey,
- what questions visitors need answered before joining,
- how Gold and Diamond benefits can be framed without overwhelming new visitors,
- how consultation, membership, and financing actions can support rather than compete with each other.

## Secondary Recommendations

- Treatment consultation flow: useful for a specific Botox, filler, or laser path.
- Review/proof placement: useful if proof can support membership or consultation actions.
- Consultation follow-up workflow: useful if the team wants to guide people after booking.

## Future Opportunities

- Lapsed-client reactivation around membership and repeat treatments.
- Seasonal membership promotion.
- Narrow AI intake for membership FAQs only after an approved script exists.

## What To Track

- Membership page clicks.
- Consultation booking clicks.
- Financing clicks, if relevant.
- Membership inquiries or signups, if available.
