# Skin Savvy Aesthetics Client Summary

I noticed one practical opportunity in the public customer journey: Skin Savvy already has a complimentary consultation path, a broad service menu, financing, and a membership offer. The opportunity is to make the membership path easier to understand in relation to the consultation step.

The first improvement I would recommend is a focused membership/package clarity module. The goal would be to help visitors understand when to book a consultation, when membership makes sense, and how Gold or Diamond benefits support repeat treatments.

This would be a focused first step, not a full site rebuild or a promise of specific results.

## Recommended First Deliverable

- Clarify the role of membership in the treatment journey.
- Connect membership to the complimentary consultation path.
- Make Gold and Diamond benefits easier to compare.
- Keep booking, membership, and financing actions from competing with each other.
