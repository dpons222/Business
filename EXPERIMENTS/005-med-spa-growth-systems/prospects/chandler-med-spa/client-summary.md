# Chandler Med Spa Client Summary

I noticed one practical opportunity in the public customer journey: the site already gives visitors ways to book or contact the med spa, but the first-page flow could make the path from treatment interest to consultation cleaner.

The first improvement I would recommend is a focused booking and page-flow cleanup. The goal would be to make one clear route for visitors who are interested in Botox, weight loss, laser hair removal, or another high-intent treatment to understand the next step and reserve a consultation.

This would be a focused first step, not a full site rebuild or a promise of specific results.

## Recommended First Deliverable

- Clarify the top consultation action.
- Reduce repeated or distracting first-page elements where possible.
- Tie one high-intent treatment path to the existing booking, call, or text options.
- Keep the recommendation based on public site content only.
