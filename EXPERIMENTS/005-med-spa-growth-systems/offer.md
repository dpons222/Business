# Med Spa Growth Systems Offer

## Offer

I inspect the med spa's public customer journey, identify the clearest consultation or repeat-client gap, and implement one focused improvement.

## Initial Offer Hypothesis

```text
Treatment booking funnel + follow-up system for one high-margin service.
```

Start with one service such as Botox, fillers, laser treatment, body contouring, aesthetic wellness, or a membership/package offer. The exact module should be chosen after reviewing the prospect's public site, booking flow, treatment pages, CTAs, proof, reviews, and follow-up path.

## Pricing Test

```text
Focused module setup: $1,250
Monthly support option: $350/month
```

## Buyer

- Owner.
- Clinic manager.
- Medical director.
- Marketing decision-maker.

## Best Fit

- Med spas with visible treatment pages and booking or consultation paths.
- Clinics with packages, memberships, promos, or repeat-client potential.
- Businesses with visible friction in treatment CTAs, proof, booking links, or follow-up expectations.

## Solution Scope

Possible first modules:

- Treatment page or landing page improvement.
- Consult request or lead capture cleanup.
- Booking CTA cleanup.
- Review and proof placement.
- Missed-call or text-back workflow.
- Email/SMS follow-up workflow.
- Chatbot or AI intake for narrow, approved intake use cases.
- AI phone follow-up for narrow reminders, reactivation, or missed-call use cases.

## Boundaries

- No medical advice, diagnosis, treatment promises, or clinical claims.
- No guaranteed bookings, patients, revenue, reviews, rankings, or ad outcomes.
- No handling private patient data in the validation phase.
- No broad AI caller deployment without a narrow approved script, clear handoff, and compliance review.
