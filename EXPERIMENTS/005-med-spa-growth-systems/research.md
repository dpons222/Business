# Med Spa Growth Systems Research

## Buyer

Owners, practice managers, marketing managers, or aesthetic clinic operators responsible for consultations, treatments, packages, reviews, and repeat visits.

## Profit Centers

- Injectables such as Botox and fillers.
- Laser hair removal packages.
- Body contouring consultations.
- Skin treatments and facials.
- Memberships and treatment packages.
- Seasonal promos and event offers.
- Repeat visits and lapsed-client reactivation.

## Common Visible Gaps

| Visible gap | Customer action affected | Business value | Possible module | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment CTAs are unclear | Consultation requests | More qualified inquiries | Treatment consultation flow cleanup | Treatment pages, booking links |
| Packages are hard to understand | Package purchase consideration | Higher average value | Package explanation module | Pricing/menu pages, FAQs |
| Follow-up expectations are missing | Consultation conversion | Better inquiry handling | Consultation follow-up workflow | Forms, thank-you flow, booking tools |
| Reviews/proof are scattered | Trust | Higher confidence | Review/testimonial workflow | Reviews, testimonials, before/after policy |
| Old clients are not visibly reactivated | Repeat bookings | Recurring revenue | Lapsed-client campaign | Email/SMS clues, promos, membership page |

## Initial Wedge

Start with consultation flow and follow-up because they are close to revenue and can be recommended from public evidence without making treatment claims.
