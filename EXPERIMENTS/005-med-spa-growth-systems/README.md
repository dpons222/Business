# 005 Med Spa Growth Systems

Validation experiment for AI-assisted growth recommendations and focused implementation modules for med spas and aesthetic clinics.

## Roadmap

Start with `roadmap.md` for the market rollout order, advancement rule, per-market checklist, and current Scottsdale / Phoenix test plan.

## Core Test

```text
Will med spas pay for a practical, personalized recommendation and implementation package based on the biggest visible gap in their consultation, treatment, package, reputation, or follow-up journey?
```

## Current Offer

```text
Primary offer: Med spa growth audit + one focused implementation module
Target market: Med spas and aesthetic clinics, starting with Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
Buyer: Owner, clinic manager, medical director, or marketing decision-maker
Price test: $1,250 setup for one focused module
Optional add-on: $350/month for campaign updates, tracking review, follow-up workflow maintenance, or content support
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Market Rollout

1. Scottsdale / Phoenix, AZ
2. Boca Raton / Fort Lauderdale / West Palm Beach, FL
3. Dallas / Plano / Frisco, TX
4. Miami, FL
5. Los Angeles / Orange County, CA

Move to the next market only after the current market reaches the advancement rule in `roadmap.md`.

## Solution Modules

- Treatment consultation flow cleanup.
- Botox/filler campaign page.
- Laser hair removal or body contouring campaign page.
- Package or membership explanation module.
- Consultation follow-up workflow.
- Review and testimonial workflow.
- Before/after proof organization with compliance caution.
- Lapsed-client reactivation campaign.
- Google Business Profile and booking link cleanup.
- Event or seasonal promo module.
- Chatbot or AI intake module for narrow, approved intake use cases.
- AI phone follow-up for narrow reminders, reactivation, or missed-call use cases.

## Key Files

- `roadmap.md`: market rollout, checklist, advancement rules, and current market notes.
- `plan.md`: experiment question, scope, checklist, and decision rules.
- `offer.md`: working offer, buyer, pricing test, and boundaries.
- `validation.md`: success metrics and market advancement rules.
- `research.md`: niche, market, buyer, and source notes.
- `marketing/`: prospect tracking, outreach, and pre-send checks.
- `product/`: solution module menu and recommendation framework.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise patients, bookings, revenue, treatment outcomes, reviews, rankings, or ad performance.
- Avoid medical claims, diagnosis, or treatment advice.
- Do not invent before/after outcomes, pricing, credentials, devices, or policies.
- Keep recommendations practical and evidence-based.
