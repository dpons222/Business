# Discovery Call Questions

- Which treatments or packages are most important to grow?
- Where do most consultation requests come from today?
- What happens after someone submits a consultation form or books online?
- Are follow-ups handled manually, through software, or not consistently?
- Are memberships, packages, or promos clear enough for new clients?
- Which proof can be used publicly with approval?
- Which single public journey would be most useful to improve first?
- What simple signals could we track without private patient data?
