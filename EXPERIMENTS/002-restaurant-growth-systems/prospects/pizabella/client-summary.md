# Pizabella Client Summary

## Recommended First Step

Start with a `Restaurant Customer Journey Cleanup`.

Your online ordering is already active, so the best first move is not to replace everything. The better first move is to clean up the public path around ordering: the website pages, menu presentation, public links, specials, and basic tracking.

## What I Noticed

- The public site has Home, About, Hours, and Order pages.
- The About page appears to still use placeholder text.
- The homepage is very short and does not show much about the restaurant, best sellers, pickup/delivery, specials, or menu highlights.
- The ordering page is active and supports pickup/delivery.
- The online menu has a lot of items, but many items appear to be missing descriptions or photos.
- Pizza Bella appears in local public directories, so menu and order links should be consistent everywhere guests find the restaurant.

## Suggested Pilot

```text
Restaurant Customer Journey Cleanup
```

This would include:

- Better Home/About/Hours copy.
- Clearer order, phone, pickup, delivery, and dine-in calls to action.
- Online ordering flow review.
- Menu description/photo recommendations for top sellers.
- Public menu/order link cleanup.
- Weekly specials workflow.
- Basic tracking recommendations.

## Why This Fits

This focuses on the pieces a guest sees before they order. It does not require a new POS, a full website rebuild, or a complex marketing campaign.

The first goal is simple:

```text
Make it easier for guests to understand what Pizza Bella offers, find the right menu/order link, and choose what to order.
```

## Future Opportunities

After the cleanup, the next best tests would be:

- a weekly specials workflow,
- review response templates,
- group order or catering inquiry page if catering is a priority,
- simple monthly tracking review.

## Boundaries

This work should not be presented as a guarantee of orders, revenue, rankings, or review growth. It is a practical cleanup and tracking pilot designed to make the guest path clearer.
