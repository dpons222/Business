## Current Read

Pizabella has a working online ordering system, but the public marketing site is thin and incomplete. The strongest first offer is not a standalone landing page. It is a paid customer journey cleanup that improves the public website, ordering path, menu content, public links, specials workflow, and basic tracking.

Do not send outreach until the contact method is verified and the pre-send checklist is completed.
