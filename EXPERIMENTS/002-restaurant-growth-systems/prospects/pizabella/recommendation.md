## Observed Issues

### 1. Public site is incomplete

The About page still uses placeholder content:

```text
About Header
About Paragraph One
About Paragraph Two
```

This is the most obvious credibility gap because visitors who want to understand the restaurant get a placeholder page instead of a real story, proof, menu highlights, or reason to order.

### 2. Homepage is too thin

The homepage primarily says:

```text
Pizza, Pasta, Subs...
and More
```

It does not surface best sellers, delivery/pickup clarity, local proof, menu highlights, specials, family/group ordering, or a stronger reason to order now.

### 3. Online ordering is active but under-merchandised

The order system appears to contain:

```text
22 categories
184 item navigation records
236 item records
1 active special
Pickup and delivery service types
Delivery fee: $2.00
Future ordering: up to 6 days
```

Observed merchandising gaps from the public order page data:

```text
107 item navigation records without descriptions
159 item navigation records without images
```

This does not mean every item needs a photo, but top sellers and high-margin items should be improved first.

### 4. Tracking and retention appear underused

The order page has analytics scripts, but the store-level custom tracking ID appears blank. Public order page data also shows rewards disabled and PWA disabled.

This creates an opportunity for basic tracking recommendations and a future retention module without changing the POS.

### 5. Public links should be audited

Pizabella appears in local directories and public listings. The Town of Woodstock links to the ArrowPOS site, and Facebook/search results point to ordering and landing pages. This makes link consistency important.

## Opportunity Stack

### Priority 1: Website/Menu Cleanup Package

Why it matters:

- The About page has placeholder copy.
- The homepage does not communicate enough value.
- Hours and phone are available but could be better connected to ordering and pickup/delivery.

Deliverables:

- Replace placeholder About content with real restaurant copy.
- Rewrite homepage hero and support copy.
- Add top menu highlights.
- Improve phone/order CTA placement.
- Clarify pickup, delivery, dine-in, and hours.
- Match brand colors from the existing ArrowPOS site.

### Priority 2: Online Ordering Conversion Audit

Why it matters:

- Online ordering is already live, so improvements can support revenue without a platform migration.
- Many menu items are missing descriptions and images.

Deliverables:

- Review menu category order and labels.
- Identify top 15-25 items needing better descriptions/photos.
- Improve featured special visibility.
- Recommend top-seller and high-margin item merchandising.
- Check order CTA clarity from Home, Hours, Facebook, and directory links.

### Priority 3: Google/Menu/Order Link Cleanup

Why it matters:

- Pizabella appears in local directories, Facebook, and tourism-style listings.
- A guest may arrive from Google, Facebook, the Town of Woodstock page, or the ArrowPOS site.

Deliverables:

- Audit public links.
- Recommend one best menu/order URL for each channel.
- Add UTM-style tracking guidance where possible.
- Create a simple owner checklist for future link updates.

### Priority 4: Weekly Specials Content System

Why it matters:

- The order page already has an active special.
- Specials can be reused across ordering, Facebook, Google Business Profile, and the homepage.

Deliverables:

- Weekly special input template.
- Reusable copy blocks for Facebook, Google, website, and ordering page.
- Approval checklist.
- Optional monthly content support.

### Priority 5: Review/Reputation Workflow

Why it matters:

- Local restaurants often win or lose guests in Google/Facebook/Yelp review surfaces.
- Review responses and reusable positive proof can support website and order-page confidence.

Deliverables:

- Review response templates.
- Weekly review checklist.
- Positive review reuse guidance.
- Escalation rules for unhappy guests.

### Priority 6: Catering / Group Order Page

Why it matters:

- Pizza, pasta, subs, wings, salads, and party-style items can support group orders.
- This should be validated before becoming the lead recommendation.

Deliverables:

- Confirm whether Pizabella wants catering/group orders.
- If yes, create a group order/catering page or inquiry flow.
- Add group-size, date/time, pickup/delivery, and menu preference fields.

## Why This Is Better Than Pitching A Landing Page First

A landing page could be useful later for specials, catering, or group orders. But the strongest visible gap is broader: the existing public presence is incomplete while the ordering system is already active. Fixing the customer journey around the ordering system is more practical and more likely to feel relevant to the owner.
