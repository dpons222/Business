# Validation

## Validation Target

Contact 20 qualified independent restaurants in one chosen market and test whether a personalized recommendation plus one focused implementation module produces meaningful interest.

## Qualified Prospect Criteria

A qualified prospect should have most of these:

- Independent restaurant or small local restaurant group.
- Public website or active Google Business Profile.
- Public menu, hours, phone, and address.
- Visible brand, food, service, review, or local proof.
- A clear gap in one customer journey area: ordering, reservations, catering, private dining, events, reviews, specials, retention, website clarity, menu clarity, or local profile links.
- A likely reachable owner, manager, catering contact, events contact, or marketing contact.

## Diagnostic Categories

Use these categories to choose the recommendation:

```text
catering_private_dining
email_sms_promo
gbp_menu_order_cleanup
online_ordering_audit
event_lead_tracking
review_response_workflow
weekly_specials_content
birthday_loyalty_reactivation
website_menu_cleanup
landing_campaign_page
```

## Disqualifiers

- No visible contact path.
- No clear business action to improve.
- Corporate chain with centralized marketing.
- Existing customer journey is already strong for the observed opportunity.
- The likely fix requires deep platform integration before a small pilot can be useful.
- The owner expects guaranteed reservations, orders, revenue, search rankings, review ratings, or ad results.

## Metrics

Track:

- Qualified prospects found.
- Diagnostic category assigned.
- Outreach messages sent.
- Positive replies.
- Neutral replies.
- Objections.
- Calls booked.
- Paid pilot commitments.
- Recommendation types that produce replies.
- Demo or recommendation page visits, if a link is sent with tracking.
- CTA clicks, inquiry submissions, or workflow usage if a pilot is launched.

## Decision Rules

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured.
- 2 serious sales calls are booked.
- 3-5 positive replies show the personalized recommendation model is worth another batch.

Narrow if:

- One module clearly gets better replies.
- Restaurants respond to the diagnosis but ask for a more specific package.
- Delivery complexity becomes too high across too many module types.

Revise if:

- Restaurants like the idea but want a lower-risk audit before implementation.
- Prospects object to price, timing, trust, or the selected module.
- Replies reveal another repeated restaurant need not listed in the current module menu.

Archive or change niche if:

- 20 qualified prospects produce no replies.
- Replies show no urgent problem or willingness to pay.
- Interest only appears when the work is free or heavily discounted.

## Evidence To Capture

For each outreach batch:

- Prospect list and qualification notes.
- Diagnostic category chosen for each restaurant.
- One-sentence recommendation for each restaurant.
- Exact outreach drafts.
- Demo or recommendation links, if any.
- Pre-send checklist evidence.
- Sent dates and follow-up dates.
- Reply summaries and objections.
- Decision after the batch.
