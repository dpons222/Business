# Assets

Use this folder for experiment-specific assets.

Examples:

- screenshots from restaurant websites,
- Google Business Profile screenshots,
- approved logos,
- approved food or dining photos,
- menu captures used for internal research,
- ordering or reservation flow screenshots,
- review workflow notes,
- brand color notes,
- demo or recommendation screenshots.

## Rules

- Do not reuse copyrighted restaurant photos in a public demo without permission.
- Keep prospect-specific assets organized by restaurant slug when added.
- Record the source of any public brand color, logo, screenshot, or flow observation.
- Use assets only to support validation and client-approved work.
