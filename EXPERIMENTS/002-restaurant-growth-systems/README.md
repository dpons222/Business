# 002 Restaurant Growth Systems

Validation experiment for personalized, AI-assisted growth recommendations for local restaurants.

## Core Test

```text
Will local restaurants pay for a practical, personalized recommendation and implementation package based on the biggest visible gap in their current customer journey?
```

## Current Offer

```text
Primary offer: Personalized restaurant growth audit + one focused implementation module
Target market: Independent local restaurants with visible marketing, ordering, booking, catering, events, reputation, or retention gaps
Price test: $750 setup for one focused module
Optional add-on: $250/month for updates, tracking review, content support, or automation maintenance
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## What This Is

This experiment is not limited to landing pages.

The goal is to inspect each restaurant and recommend the most useful next improvement. A landing page may be the right answer for one restaurant. Another restaurant may need menu/order link cleanup, a private dining inquiry flow, a review response workflow, weekly specials content, or a birthday/loyalty reactivation campaign.

## Solution Modules

- Catering or private dining inquiry page.
- Restaurant email/SMS promo automation.
- Google Business Profile, menu, and ordering link cleanup.
- Online ordering conversion audit.
- Event or private dining lead tracking system.
- Review response and reputation workflow.
- Weekly specials content automation.
- Birthday, loyalty, or lapsed-guest reactivation campaign.
- Restaurant website and menu cleanup package.
- Focused landing page or campaign page when that is the best fit.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed reservations, orders, catering leads, revenue, search rankings, review ratings, or ad performance.
- Do not force every restaurant into the same service.
- Match the recommendation to the restaurant's actual public customer journey.
- Keep unpaid personalized demos light until there is interest.
- Match restaurant logo and site colors when creating any visual demo.
- Use public restaurant facts carefully, and do not imply endorsement before the restaurant approves the work.
- Before sending any outreach, run the marketing pre-send checklist in `marketing/README.md`.
