# Discovery Call Questions

Use these when a restaurant owner, manager, catering contact, events contact, or marketing contact shows interest.

## Priority

- Which area matters most right now: orders, reservations, catering, private dining, events, reviews, weekly specials, email/SMS, loyalty, or website clarity?
- What made that area a priority?
- How do guests currently find and complete that action?
- Where do you think people get confused or drop off?

## Current Flow

- What page, platform, link, form, phone number, or internal process handles this today?
- Which links do you currently use from Google, Instagram, email, QR cards, or ads?
- What questions do guests usually ask before they book, order, inquire, or visit?
- Are there outdated links, menus, hours, policies, or offers that need cleanup?

## Module Fit

- Would a focused page, link cleanup, inquiry flow, review workflow, content template, or follow-up sequence be most useful first?
- What would be too much for a first pilot?
- What platforms or tools should not be touched?
- Are there any brand, menu, offer, or policy rules I should follow?

## Content

- Which menu items, packages, offers, events, reviews, or photos should be highlighted?
- Do you have approved photos we can use?
- Are there brand colors, fonts, or logos that should guide any visual work?
- Are there capacity limits, timing rules, lead-time requirements, or blackout dates?

## Operations

- Who receives inquiries or manages this process?
- What details do you need from a guest before you can respond?
- How quickly can your team respond?
- What should happen after someone clicks, calls, orders, reserves, or submits a form?

## Tracking

- What would make this pilot worth continuing?
- Do you care most about calls, inquiry forms, reservation clicks, order clicks, email signups, review responses, repeat visits, or another signal?
- Do you already track website clicks, calls, reservations, orders, reviews, or email/SMS activity?
- How often would you want a short performance review?

## Close

- If I recommend one first improvement, who needs to approve it?
- What would make you comfortable starting with a fixed-scope pilot?
- Are there any platforms, links, or brand rules I should know before proposing the first module?
