# Outreach Script

## First Message: Personalized Recommendation

Subject:

```text
Quick idea for [Restaurant Name]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

My name is Diego. I'm with DigiDap, where I help local service businesses improve their websites and turn high-intent pages into clearer customer inquiry paths.

I was looking at [Restaurant Name] and noticed [specific public detail: menu/order link, private dining, catering, reviews, weekly specials, Google link, events, etc.].

One practical idea: [one-sentence recommendation].

I am testing a fixed-scope service for local restaurants where I review the public guest journey, pick the clearest opportunity, and implement one focused improvement. For some restaurants that is a campaign page. For others it is order link cleanup, an inquiry flow, review response workflow, weekly specials content, or a simple follow-up system.

Would it be useful if I sent over a quick example direction for [Restaurant Name]?

Diego
```

## First Message: Module-Specific

Use this when the best module is obvious.

Subject:

```text
Idea for [specific opportunity] at [Restaurant Name]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

My name is Diego. I'm with DigiDap, where I help local service businesses improve their websites and turn high-intent pages into clearer customer inquiry paths.

I noticed [specific public detail].

The opportunity I see is [diagnosis]. A focused fix could be [recommended module], so guests have a clearer path to [desired action].

I am testing this as a small fixed-scope service for local restaurants. It would use your existing public information, links, and brand direction rather than replacing your whole website.

Worth sending over a quick example direction?

Diego
```

## Follow-Up 1

Send 3-5 business days after the first message.

```text
Hi [Name],

Quick follow-up on the idea for [Restaurant Name].

The reason I thought it might fit is [specific observed issue or opportunity]. I would keep the first step narrow: [recommended solution].

Worth a quick look, or should I leave it?

Diego
```

## Follow-Up 2

Send 5-7 business days after follow-up 1.

```text
Hi [Name],

Last note from me.

If [observed opportunity] becomes a priority later, I think [Restaurant Name] could test it with one focused improvement and simple tracking before committing to anything bigger.

Thanks for taking a look.

Diego
```

## Contact Form Version

Use this when the contact form is short.

```text
Hi, my name is Diego. I'm with DigiDap, where I help local service businesses improve their websites and turn high-intent pages into clearer customer inquiry paths. I had a practical idea for [Restaurant Name]: [one-sentence recommendation]. I am testing a fixed-scope service where I review a local restaurant's public guest journey and implement one focused improvement, such as order link cleanup, an inquiry flow, a campaign page, review workflow, weekly specials content, or simple follow-up automation. If useful, I can send a quick example direction. - Diego
```

## Guardrails

- Mention one real observed opportunity.
- Keep the message short.
- Do not imply the restaurant has a broken business.
- Do not force the same recommendation on every prospect.
- Do not promise reservations, orders, catering leads, revenue, rankings, review ratings, or ad performance.
- Do not send a demo link until the page or recommendation is reviewed and the pre-send checklist is complete.
