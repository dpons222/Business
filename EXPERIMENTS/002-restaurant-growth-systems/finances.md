# Finances

## Price Test

```text
Setup price: $750 for one focused implementation module
Optional monthly add-on: $250/month
```

## Setup Scope Assumption

The setup price assumes:

- One diagnostic review.
- One prioritized recommendation.
- One implementation module.
- Existing restaurant assets and public information are enough for a first version.
- No complex integrations beyond links, docs, templates, lightweight forms, spreadsheets, or existing platform settings.
- One revision round.

## Monthly Scope Assumption

The optional monthly add-on covers one narrow support lane, such as:

- minor page, link, or copy edits,
- seasonal campaign updates,
- weekly specials content support,
- email/SMS promo maintenance,
- review workflow updates,
- tracking review,
- a short performance summary,
- one recommended next test per month.

## Expected Costs

Likely direct costs per unpaid validation batch:

- Domain: $0 if using existing preview domain or docs only.
- Hosting: $0 incremental if using existing Vercel setup or static docs only.
- Email: $0 incremental for manual outreach or approved existing automation.
- Research time: primary cost.
- Demo/recommendation personalization time: keep under 5-10 minutes before a positive reply.

Likely direct costs per paid pilot:

- Hosting: low or covered by existing infrastructure.
- Analytics: low or included in current tools.
- Email/SMS tools: client should provide or approve any paid account.
- Stock or paid images: avoid unless the client approves.
- Automation tools: client should approve any paid tools.

## Break-Even Notes

At $750 setup:

- 1 paid pilot validates willingness to pay and funds more testing.
- 2 paid pilots can justify building reusable templates for the winning modules.
- Monthly add-ons become meaningful only if clients value ongoing updates, content, workflow maintenance, tracking checks, or campaign support.

## Assumptions

- Restaurants are more likely to pay when the recommendation is specific to their current customer journey.
- The strongest module may vary by restaurant segment.
- A light personalized recommendation can improve reply quality, but unpaid customization must remain limited.
- Over time, the experiment should narrow around the modules that produce replies and paid pilots.

## Financial Guardrails

- Do not discount below the point where setup time becomes unsustainable.
- Do not include custom integrations in the base setup price.
- Do not absorb paid photo, ad, reservation, POS, ordering, SMS, or email platform costs unless the client approves them.
- Do not sell ongoing reporting unless tracking exists and the client understands what is being reviewed.
