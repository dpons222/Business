# Solution Modules

## 1. Emergency Repair Call Path Cleanup

Best for urgent-service companies with unclear mobile phone CTAs, weak emergency messaging, or buried service pages.

Deliverables: CTA cleanup recommendations, emergency copy blocks, mobile header guidance, tracking plan.

## 2. AC / Furnace Replacement Quote Page

Best for companies with high-value replacement services but generic or thin replacement pages.

Deliverables: page copy, FAQ, trust proof structure, financing CTA, quote request flow.

## 3. Seasonal Tune-Up Campaign Page

Best for companies with maintenance demand, seasonal specials, or tune-up offers.

Deliverables: campaign copy, offer framing, CTA, follow-up plan, tracking signals.

## 4. Financing And Rebate Clarity Module

Best for replacement-focused companies where financing, rebates, or payment options affect decisions.

Deliverables: explanation section, FAQ, CTA placement, proof checklist.

## 5. Missed-Lead Follow-Up Workflow

Best for companies with forms, quote requests, or after-hours inquiries.

Deliverables: response expectations, email/SMS follow-up copy, status tracker, approval checklist.

## 6. Maintenance Plan Signup Flow

Best for companies with recurring service plans that are hard to find or hard to understand.

Deliverables: plan explanation, signup CTA, comparison copy, follow-up sequence.

## 7. Service Area And Local Proof Cleanup

Best for companies with scattered local proof or unclear service areas.

Deliverables: service area copy, proof placement, review/testimonial usage guidance.

## 8. Review Capture And Reputation Workflow

Best for active service companies with inconsistent review responses or underused reviews.

Deliverables: response templates, request timing, internal checklist, approved proof reuse guidance.
