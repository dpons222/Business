# HVAC Growth Systems Finances

## Price Test

```text
Setup: $1,000
Monthly support: $300/month
```

## Delivery Assumptions

Starter module delivery target:

- 4-8 hours for diagnosis, copy, recommendation, and lightweight implementation assets.
- 1-2 hours for follow-up edits.
- Minimal software costs unless the client requires a specific tool.

## Simple Revenue Scenarios

| Scenario | Setup clients/month | Monthly clients | Estimated monthly revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | 1 | 0 | $1,000 |

| Strong | 4 | 5 | $5,500 |

## Margin Notes

This niche can justify higher setup pricing if the module is tied to replacement, emergency repair, or maintenance plan value. Avoid performance guarantees.
