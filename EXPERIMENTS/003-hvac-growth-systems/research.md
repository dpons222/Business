# HVAC Growth Systems Research

## Buyer

Residential HVAC owners, general managers, marketing managers, or office managers responsible for service calls, replacement leads, tune-up campaigns, and customer follow-up.

## Profit Centers

- Emergency AC or furnace repair calls.
- AC replacement and furnace replacement estimates.
- Seasonal tune-ups and maintenance plan signups.
- Financing-assisted replacement projects.
- Indoor air quality add-ons.
- Ductless mini-split consultations.
- Repeat service and warranty follow-up.

## Common Visible Gaps

| Visible gap | Customer action affected | Business value | Possible module | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency CTA buried or unclear | Urgent calls | Higher-value fast-response jobs | Emergency repair call path cleanup | Homepage, mobile header, service pages |
| Replacement page is generic | Estimate requests | Large-ticket installs | Replacement quote page | AC/furnace pages, financing mentions |
| Tune-up offer missing or seasonal only | Maintenance signups | Recurring service and retention | Seasonal tune-up campaign page | Specials, maintenance pages, GBP posts |
| Financing or rebate info unclear | Replacement decision | Reduces buyer hesitation | Financing and rebate clarity module | Financing page, CTA copy, FAQs |
| Forms do not show follow-up expectations | Quote requests | Faster lead handling | Missed-lead follow-up workflow | Forms, thank-you flow, email/SMS clues |
| Service area proof is thin | Local trust | Better fit for nearby homeowners | Service area and local proof cleanup | Location pages, reviews, testimonials |

## Channel Assumptions

HVAC prospects can be found through Google local search, Google Ads visibility, service area pages, local sponsor listings, review profiles, and home services directories.

## Initial Wedge

Start with emergency repair and replacement quote flow. These are close to revenue, easy for owners to understand, and visible from public pages.
