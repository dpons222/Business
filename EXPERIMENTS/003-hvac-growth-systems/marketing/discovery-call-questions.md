# Discovery Call Questions

## Fit

- Which services are most valuable right now: emergency repair, replacement, maintenance, financing-assisted installs, or something else?
- Where do most new inquiries come from today?
- Which inquiries are hardest to respond to consistently?

## Current Flow

- What happens after someone submits a form or calls after hours?
- Do replacement estimates have a different follow-up process than repair calls?
- Are tune-up or maintenance plan campaigns seasonal or ongoing?

## Offer Fit

- If we fixed one visible public journey first, which one would matter most?
- Would a focused module be more useful than a larger redesign right now?
- Who would need to approve a pilot?

## Measurement

- What simple signals can you share: calls, form submissions, booked estimates, or follow-ups sent?
- What would make the first 30 days feel worth continuing?
