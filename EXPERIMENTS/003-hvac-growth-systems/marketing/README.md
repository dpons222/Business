# Marketing

Use this folder for HVAC prospect research, outreach, validation tracking, and batch review.

## Files

- `prospect-tracker.csv`: active prospect tracker.
- `prospect-tracker.md`: tracker field guide and status values.
- `outreach-script.md`: first message, follow-ups, and channel guidance.
- `discovery-call-questions.md`: questions for interested HVAC owners or managers.

## Rule

Lead with one observed opportunity from the company's public customer journey. Do not send generic "we build websites" or "we automate follow-up" outreach.

## Pre-Send Checklist

- Verify company name, service area, website, and contact method.
- Re-check the exact page or flow mentioned in the draft.
- Confirm the recommendation category is specific.
- Confirm the message avoids promises about calls, jobs, rankings, reviews, revenue, or ads.
- Confirm tracker status and next follow-up date.
