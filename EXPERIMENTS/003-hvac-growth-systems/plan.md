# HVAC Growth Systems Plan

## Experiment Question

Will residential HVAC companies pay for one focused growth improvement selected from their visible customer journey?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: HVAC customer journey audit + one focused implementation module
First modules to test: emergency repair call path, replacement quote page, missed-lead follow-up, seasonal tune-up campaign
Target market: HVAC companies serving residential homeowners
Price test: $1,000 setup
Optional add-on: $300/month
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

HVAC companies lose value when urgent repair calls, replacement inquiries, financing questions, tune-up offers, and follow-ups are spread across unclear pages, forms, phone numbers, and manual processes. A narrow, visible fix can be easier to sell than a broad website redesign.

## Scope

In scope:

- Define HVAC-specific profit centers and customer actions.
- Create a flexible solution module menu.
- Research 20 qualified HVAC prospects later.
- Create outreach and discovery assets.
- Track replies, objections, and module interest.

Out of scope:

- Building coded demos.
- Running ads.
- Managing dispatch, pricing, warranty, or job scheduling systems.
- Guaranteeing calls, booked jobs, replacements, revenue, rankings, reviews, or ad results.
