# 003 HVAC Growth Systems

Validation experiment for AI-assisted growth recommendations and focused implementation modules for HVAC companies.

## Core Test

```text
Will HVAC companies pay for a practical, personalized recommendation and implementation package based on the biggest visible gap in their emergency, replacement, maintenance, or quote-request customer journey?
```

## Current Offer

```text
Primary offer: HVAC growth audit + one focused implementation module
Target market: Residential HVAC companies with visible service, replacement, maintenance, financing, or lead follow-up gaps
Price test: $1,000 setup for one focused module
Optional add-on: $300/month for tracking review, seasonal updates, follow-up workflow maintenance, or content support
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Solution Modules

- Emergency repair call path cleanup.
- AC or furnace replacement quote page.
- Seasonal tune-up campaign page.
- Financing and rebate clarity module.
- Missed-call and form follow-up workflow.
- Maintenance plan signup flow.
- Service area and local proof cleanup.
- Review capture and reputation response workflow.
- Google Business Profile service/link cleanup.
- Estimate pipeline tracker and follow-up scripts.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed calls, booked jobs, replacements, maintenance plan signups, rankings, reviews, revenue, or ad performance.
- Match each recommendation to the company's public customer journey.
- Keep unpaid examples light until there is interest.
- Mark contact methods as unverified until manually checked.
