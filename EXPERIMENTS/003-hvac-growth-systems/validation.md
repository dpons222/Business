# HVAC Growth Systems Validation

## Qualification Criteria

Qualified prospects should have:

- residential HVAC services,
- visible service area,
- working website,
- clear emergency, replacement, maintenance, or quote-request opportunity,
- public signs that one focused improvement could help.

## Validation Metrics

Track:

- prospects researched,
- recommendation category,
- contact method verified,
- outreach sent,
- positive replies,
- objections,
- calls booked,
- paid pilots,
- most requested modules.

## Success Criteria

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured,
- 2 serious sales calls are booked,
- or 3-5 positive replies show interest in a focused HVAC growth module.

Revise if:

- HVAC owners prefer a different module than the first wedge.
- Price is the main objection but pain is real.
- They want maintenance, financing, or lead follow-up more than emergency/replacement pages.

Stop if:

- 20 qualified prospects produce no replies.
- Replies show no urgent pain or willingness to pay.
- Interest only appears when work is free.
