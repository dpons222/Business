# 006 Dental Implant Cosmetic Growth Systems

Validation experiment for AI-assisted growth recommendations and focused implementation modules for dental implant and cosmetic dentistry practices.

## Core Test

```text
Will dental implant or cosmetic dentistry practices pay for a practical, personalized recommendation and implementation package based on the biggest visible gap in their consultation, treatment, financing, proof, or follow-up journey?
```

## Current Offer

```text
Primary offer: Dental growth audit + one focused implementation module
Target market: Dental practices promoting implants, Invisalign, veneers, cosmetic dentistry, whitening, or high-value treatment consultations
Price test: $1,250 setup for one focused module
Optional add-on: $350/month for campaign updates, tracking review, follow-up workflow maintenance, or content support
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Solution Modules

- Implant consultation flow cleanup.
- Cosmetic dentistry consultation page.
- Invisalign or clear aligner campaign page.
- Financing and treatment cost clarity module.
- Unscheduled consultation follow-up workflow.
- Review and smile-gallery proof organization.
- New patient inquiry path cleanup.
- Google Business Profile and booking link cleanup.
- Treatment FAQ and trust module.
- Consultation pipeline tracker and scripts.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise patients, appointments, cases, treatment outcomes, revenue, rankings, reviews, or ad performance.
- Avoid medical or dental advice.
- Do not invent credentials, treatment results, prices, financing terms, or before/after proof.
- Do not handle private patient data in the validation phase.
