# Dental Implant Cosmetic Growth Systems Validation

## Qualification Criteria

Qualified prospects should have:

- implant or cosmetic treatment pages,
- a public consultation, booking, call, or form path,
- visible proof, financing, or follow-up opportunity,
- no need for private patient data to make a first recommendation.

## Metrics

Track prospects researched, recommendation category, contact method, outreach sent, replies, objections, calls booked, paid pilots, and module interest.

## Success Criteria

Continue with 1 paid pilot, 2 serious calls, or 3-5 positive replies. Revise if practices prefer financing clarity, follow-up, or proof organization over treatment pages. Stop if 20 qualified prospects show no credible pain.
