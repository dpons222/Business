# Dental Implant Cosmetic Growth Systems Research

## Buyer

Practice owners, dentists, office managers, marketing managers, or treatment coordinators responsible for high-value dental consultations.

## Profit Centers

- Dental implant consultations.
- All-on-X or full-arch implant consults.
- Invisalign or clear aligner starts.
- Veneers and smile makeover consultations.
- Cosmetic bonding and whitening.
- New patient exams tied to cosmetic treatment.
- Financing-assisted treatment plans.

## Common Visible Gaps

| Visible gap | Customer action affected | Business value | Possible module | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Implant CTA is unclear | Consultation requests | High-value treatment inquiries | Implant consultation flow cleanup | Implant page, CTA, forms |
| Financing is vague | Treatment consideration | Reduces hesitation | Financing and cost clarity module | Financing page, FAQ, payment copy |
| Proof is scattered | Trust and comparison | Stronger patient confidence | Review and smile-gallery proof organization | Reviews, testimonials, gallery |
| Follow-up expectations missing | Unscheduled consults | Better lead handling | Unscheduled consultation follow-up | Forms, booking flow, thank-you copy |
| Treatment page is generic | Specific treatment inquiries | Clearer fit | Cosmetic consult campaign page | Veneers/Invisalign/cosmetic pages |

## Initial Wedge

Start with implant consultation flow and financing clarity because these are close to high-value decisions and visible from public pages.
