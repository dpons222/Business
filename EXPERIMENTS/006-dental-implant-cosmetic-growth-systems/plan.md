# Dental Implant Cosmetic Growth Systems Plan

## Experiment Question

Will implant and cosmetic dental practices pay for one focused growth improvement selected from their visible consultation, treatment, financing, proof, or follow-up journey?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Dental customer journey audit + one focused implementation module
First modules to test: implant consultation flow, financing clarity, cosmetic consult page, unscheduled lead follow-up
Target market: Practices marketing implants, Invisalign, veneers, whitening, or cosmetic dentistry
Price test: $1,250 setup
Optional add-on: $350/month
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

High-value dental treatment leads need trust, clarity, financing context, and fast follow-up. Many practices have treatment pages, but the consultation path, proof, and next steps are not always clear enough for patients comparing options.
