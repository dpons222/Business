# Recommendation Framework

## Diagnostic Flow

1. Identify high-value treatment pages and consultation paths.
2. Review implant, Invisalign, cosmetic, financing, review, and booking surfaces.
3. Look for visible friction that can be fixed without private patient data.
4. Choose one recommendation category.
5. Keep the recommendation practical and non-clinical.

## Selection Rules

Choose `implant_consultation_flow` when implant pages exist but the next step is unclear.

Choose `financing_cost_clarity` when cost or financing is likely important but vague.

Choose `unscheduled_consult_followup` when forms exist but response expectations are unclear.

Choose `review_smile_gallery_proof` when proof exists but is scattered or underused.

## Recommendation Format

```text
Observed: [specific public issue or opportunity].
Recommendation: [one focused improvement].
Why it fits: [dental-specific reason].
First deliverable: [one module].
What to track: [one to three simple non-private signals].
```
