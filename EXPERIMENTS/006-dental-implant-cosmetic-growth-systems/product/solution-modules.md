# Solution Modules

## 1. Implant Consultation Flow Cleanup

Clarifies the path from implant interest to consultation request.

## 2. Cosmetic Dentistry Consultation Page

Creates a focused path for veneers, whitening, bonding, or smile makeover interest.

## 3. Invisalign Or Clear Aligner Campaign Page

Frames one treatment category with FAQs, trust proof, CTA, and tracking plan.

## 4. Financing And Treatment Cost Clarity Module

Improves public explanation of payment options without inventing prices or terms.

## 5. Unscheduled Consultation Follow-Up Workflow

Defines follow-up copy, response expectations, and status tracking for inquiries.

## 6. Review And Smile-Gallery Proof Organization

Organizes approved proof and review themes conservatively.

## 7. New Patient Inquiry Path Cleanup

Improves the path from local search or website visit to booking or calling.

## 8. Consultation Pipeline Tracker

Creates a simple tracker for inquiry status, follow-up, and next steps.
