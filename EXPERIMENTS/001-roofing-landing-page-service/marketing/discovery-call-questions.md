# Discovery Call Questions

## Goal

Understand whether the roofer has a real landing-page problem, existing traffic, and willingness to pay for a focused conversion asset.

## Questions

```text
How are you currently getting storm damage or roof inspection leads?

Are you running Google Ads, Local Services Ads, SEO, Facebook, directories, or buying leads?

Do you currently send traffic to a dedicated storm damage / inspection page?

What happens when someone fills out a form or calls from your website?

What is a qualified roof inspection lead worth to you?

What is the biggest problem with your current website or landing pages?

Do you know your current conversion rate from visitors to calls/forms?

What service would you most want a landing page for: storm damage, roof repair, replacement, emergency leak, or inspection?

What proof matters most to homeowners in your market: reviews, licenses, insurance help, before/after photos, warranties, financing, local experience?

If I built a focused page for $1,000, what would need to be included for that to feel worth testing?

Would you want ongoing tracking and small improvements for $300/month, or just the one-time page?
```

## Qualification Signals

Strong signals:

- They already spend money to get leads.
- They can describe their ideal lead.
- They care about calls, forms, and inspections.
- Their current website is weak or generic.
- They understand one job can justify the test.

Weak signals:

- No marketing activity.
- No reviews or trust proof.
- No clear service focus.
- Wants guaranteed leads.
- Wants free work before any payment.
