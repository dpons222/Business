# First Outreach Batch

## Purpose

Shortlist the first 10 roofing prospects for outreach after a first-pass website audit.

These are not deep conversion audits. They are practical outreach notes: enough to write a specific message without doing unpaid custom strategy work.

## Selection Criteria

Selected prospects had most of the following:

- Publicly reachable website page
- Texas service area
- Storm, hail, roof inspection, or roof damage content
- Existing CTA or form that could plausibly be improved
- Specific enough issue to mention in outreach
- Clear contact path

## Batch Summary

```text
Prospects reviewed for shortlist: 30
First outreach batch: 10
Primary angle: storm damage / roof inspection landing page
Outreach goal: 3-5 positive replies or 1 paid pilot
```

## Notes Before Sending

- Do a quick live visual check in a normal browser before contacting each prospect.
- Do not imply we fully audited their site.
- Keep the message short and specific.
- Avoid calling their site "bad" or "weak."
- Frame the idea as a focused landing page test, not a full website redesign.

## Suggested First-Batch Message Pattern

```text
Hi [Name],

I was reviewing roofing companies in [City] and noticed [specific observation].

I am testing a focused landing page offer for roofers: a storm damage / roof inspection page designed to turn more visitors into calls or inspection requests.

For your site, my first thought was: [personalized improvement idea].

Best,
[Your Name]
```
