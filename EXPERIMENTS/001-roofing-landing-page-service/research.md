## Key Research Takeaways

- Roofing contractors are a strong starting niche because lead values are high and conversion improvements can be easy to explain.
- Storm damage and roof inspection pages create a clear homeowner action: call, request inspection, or request estimate.
- Roofing businesses are easy to find through Google Maps, local search, directories, and Facebook.
- The first offer should stay narrow and avoid broad web design positioning.
- Claims must avoid guarantees around leads, rankings, revenue, or insurance outcomes.
