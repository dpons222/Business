# Personalized Demo Template

## Purpose

Create a lightweight, repeatable demo format for showing a roofing prospect how the base storm damage / roof inspection landing page could look with their business details.

This is not a full custom build. It is a fast personalization layer for outreach or follow-up.

## Time Limit

```text
Maximum time per prospect before reply: 5-10 minutes
```

If it takes longer, use the base copy deck and send a short audit note instead.

## Headline Formula

Default:

```text
Storm Damage? Schedule a Free Roof Inspection with [companyName].
```

City-specific:

```text
[city] Storm Damage? Schedule a Free Roof Inspection with [companyName].
```

Service-specific:

```text
Need a [primaryService]? Schedule a Free Inspection with [companyName].
```

## Supporting Copy Formula

Default:

```text
If hail, wind, or heavy rain hit your area, [companyName] can inspect your roof, document visible damage, and explain clear next steps before any work begins.
```

City-specific:

```text
If storms recently hit [city] or nearby neighborhoods, [companyName] can inspect your roof, document visible damage, and explain clear next steps before any work begins.
```

## Trust Line Formula

Use only verified public proof.

With rating/reviews:

```text
Serving [serviceArea] homeowners. [rating] rating from [reviewCount] reviews.
```

With trust signal:

```text
Serving [serviceArea] homeowners. [trustSignal].
```

Fallback:

```text
Serving homeowners throughout [serviceArea] with roof inspections, storm damage repair, and roof replacement support.
```

## Recommended CTA Options

Use one primary CTA.

```text
Schedule Free Roof Inspection
Request Free Hail Damage Inspection
Book Free Storm Damage Inspection
Get My Free Inspection Today
Talk To A Roofer
```

## Guardrails

- Do not publish personalized demos publicly without permission.
- Do not imply the company approved or endorsed the demo.
- Do not fabricate ratings, reviews, certifications, or trust badges.
- Do not use copyrighted photos or logos in public materials without permission.
- Do not promise leads, rankings, revenue, or insurance outcomes.
