# Final Cut Roofing Personalized Demo Brief

## Purpose

Create a lightweight personalized demo direction for Final Cut Roofing before any code is written.

This brief should support outreach by showing a clear page concept tailored to Final Cut Roofing without doing a full custom build upfront.

```text
Company: Final Cut Roofing

Market: Frisco / DFW, TX
Phone: 972-777-0350
Primary service angle: Free storm / hail damage roof inspection
Recommended CTA: Schedule Free Roof Inspection
Priority: High
```

## Source Page

```text
https://finalcutroofing.com/free-roof-inspection/
```

## Why This Prospect Fits

- Strong match for a storm damage / inspection landing page.
- Existing page already mentions hail damage and free roof inspection.
- Existing page references nearby DFW cities including Frisco, McKinney, Plano, The Colony, and Denton.
- Has visible proof: "Excellent" based on 29 reviews.
- Has a clear phone number and request estimate flow.

## Observed Opportunity

The current page has the right offer, but the request flow may create friction for a storm visitor.

Specific notes:

- The request form asks for many fields before submission.
- The form includes emergency status, referral source, full address, story count, service interests, details, and CAPTCHA.
- Service interest choices include many non-roofing options such as siding, fencing, gutters, painting, flooring, cabinets, drywall, and Christmas lights.
- A homeowner coming from a hail/storm page may need a shorter path to request a roof inspection.

## Recommended Page Angle

Create a shorter storm-inspection landing page that:

- Keeps the hail-specific message.
- Makes the free inspection CTA obvious immediately.
- Reduces the form to fewer required fields.
- Moves trust proof close to the CTA.
- Focuses on roof inspection instead of broad exterior-service choices.
- Gives homeowners one clear next step after a storm.

## What Not To Include In The Demo

- Do not include unverified badges, certifications, or awards.
- Do not use Final Cut Roofing images or logo in public materials without permission.
- Do not claim Final Cut Roofing approved the demo.
- Do not promise guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, or insurance outcomes.
- Do not include broad service choices in the first demo form.
