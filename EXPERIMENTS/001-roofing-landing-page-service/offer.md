# Offer

## Working Offer

```text
I build conversion-focused storm damage / roof inspection landing pages for roofing companies that want more calls, quote requests, and booked inspections from their existing traffic.
```

## Package

```text
Setup price: $1,000
Optional monthly add-on: $300/month
```

## Setup Includes

- One landing page focused on storm damage / roof inspection leads.
- Conversion-focused copy.
- Clear call buttons and quote / inspection request form.
- Trust sections for reviews, licenses, service areas, and proof.
- Mobile-first layout guidance.
- Basic tracking recommendations.
- One revision round.

## Optional Monthly Add-On

- Small copy or section edits.
- Tracking review.
- Form and call button checks.
- Speed and mobile usability checks.
- Simple monthly performance summary.
- Basic conversion test recommendations.

## Positioning

This should be sold as a revenue and conversion asset, not as a generic web design service.

Core message:

```text
Your storm damage traffic is expensive. The landing page should make it easy for homeowners to request an inspection or call quickly.
```

## Primary Buyer

Small-to-mid-sized roofing companies that:

- Operate in storm-prone areas.
- Have reviews and visible proof of past work.
- Already care about online leads.
- Have weak service pages or no dedicated storm damage page.
- May be paying for ads, SEO, directories, or lead providers.

## Avoid

- Brand-new roofers with no reviews.
- Large franchises with established marketing teams.
- Companies with no apparent online marketing activity.
- Companies asking for guaranteed leads, rankings, or insurance claim outcomes.

## Offer Boundaries

Do not promise:

- Guaranteed leads.
- Guaranteed rankings.
- Guaranteed booked jobs.
- Insurance claim outcomes.
- Legal or compliance advice.

Do promise:

- A clearer landing page.
- Better conversion-focused structure.
- Faster path to request an inspection.
- Stronger trust presentation.
- Better alignment between traffic and call to action.
