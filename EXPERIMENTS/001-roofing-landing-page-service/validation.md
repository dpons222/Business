# Validation

## Validation Target

```text
Prospects: 30 roofing companies
Timebox: 10 days
Success signal: 1 paid pilot or 3-5 positive replies
```

## Prospect Criteria

A qualified prospect should match most of these:

- Roofing company in a storm-prone U.S. state.
- Has a working website.
- Has visible reviews or testimonials.
- Offers storm damage, roof inspection, roof repair, or roof replacement.
- Has a weak or generic service page.
- Does not appear to have a dedicated conversion-focused landing page.
- Has a contact form, email, Facebook page, or other reachable channel.

## Decision Rule

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured, or
- 3-5 positive replies are received, or
- 1-2 serious calls are booked.

Revise if:

- Interest exists but the offer angle is wrong.
- Price is the main objection.
- Prospects prefer a different service page.
- Prospects want a website audit first.

Stop or change niche if:

- 30 qualified prospects produce no meaningful replies.
- The pain is not obvious.
- Prospects only want free work.
