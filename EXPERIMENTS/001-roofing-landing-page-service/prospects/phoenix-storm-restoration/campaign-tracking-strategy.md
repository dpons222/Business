# Phoenix Storm Restoration Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Campaign Goal

Test whether a focused Fort Worth emergency storm inspection page converts storm-concerned homeowners better than a broader service-area page.

## Suggested Channels

- Google Business Profile posts after weather events.
- Local ads for emergency roof inspection and tarping.
- QR or SMS follow-up after inbound calls.
- Retargeting from the service-area page.

## Track

- Page visits.
- Phone CTA clicks.
- Contact form submissions.
- Source channel.
- Inspection requests marked as storm/emergency related.

## Notes

Do not promise arrival time, claim outcomes, or emergency availability beyond what Phoenix confirms.
