# Rivertop Roofing Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why Rivertop Roofing should test a focused Plano storm damage / free roof inspection landing page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is a campaign page for homeowners who recently searched after hail, wind, or severe weather.

## Current Page Strengths

- Rivertop already has a dedicated Plano roof inspection page.
- The page clearly says roof inspections are free.
- The page includes a detailed inspection checklist.
- The page offers a 12-month weather impact history and color photo report.
- Public trust proof includes Yelp Guaranteed, Google reviews, BBB, and local reputation language.
- Phone contact is visible: `(888) 909-5424`.

Source reviewed June 24, 2026:

```text
https://www.rivertoproofing.com/plano-tx-roof-inspection
```

## Conversion Opportunity

The page covers several use cases before the visitor reaches the CTA:

- storm damage,
- real estate inspection,
- buyer/seller use cases,
- insurance restoration,
- broad roof condition education.

A tighter storm-specific page could make the action clearer for homeowners who just had hail, wind, or tree damage and want to know whether they need a roof inspection.

## Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your page is too long.
```

Say:

```text
Your Plano inspection page already has strong proof and a detailed inspection offer. I think a storm-specific version could make the next step clearer for homeowners after hail or wind.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
Storm Damage in Plano? Book a Free Roof Inspection.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Book Free Plano Roof Inspection
```

## Suggested Pilot Offer

```text
A focused Plano storm inspection landing page that keeps Rivertop's free inspection offer, phone path, weather impact report, and photo-report proof, but makes the next step easier to act on and measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, or booked jobs.
