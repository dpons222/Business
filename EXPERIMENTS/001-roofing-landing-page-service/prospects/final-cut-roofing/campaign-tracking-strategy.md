# Final Cut Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Purpose

The landing page should be tested with one focused campaign at a time. The goal is to see whether a clearer storm-damage inspection page turns more visitors into inspection requests.

## What A Focused Campaign Means

```text
Campaign: Frisco storm-damage roof inspection
Audience: Homeowners in Frisco and nearby DFW cities
Traffic source: one selected channel
Landing page: Final Cut Roofing storm inspection page
Goal: free roof inspection requests
Tracking: visits, form submissions, click-to-call activity, and booked inspections
```

The page should not be judged in isolation. It should be judged by how well it converts traffic from a specific source.

## Responsibility Levels

### Level 1: Landing Page Only

Final Cut Roofing chooses where to share the landing page. We build the page and set up basic tracking.

Possible uses:

- Google Business Profile link
- Facebook post
- Instagram bio or story link
- SMS follow-up link
- sales team follow-up link
- QR code on door hangers, mailers, or leave-behind material

This is the easiest version to sell because it does not require ad management.

### Level 2: Landing Page + Campaign Support

We help Final Cut choose one campaign channel and prepare the page link, tracking links, and basic message copy.

Possible support:

- Google Business Profile campaign link
- Facebook or Instagram post copy
- SMS follow-up message
- QR code destination
- UTM tracking links
- simple 30-day campaign review

This is the recommended starting point because it keeps the offer practical while still helping the client test the page properly.

### Level 3: Landing Page + Ad Management

We actively manage paid traffic, such as Google Ads or Facebook/Instagram ads.

This adds responsibility for:

- ad budget recommendations
- targeting
- ad creative
- keywords
- conversion tracking
- campaign performance
- ongoing optimization

This should not be the first offer unless paid ad management becomes a deliberate service.

## Recommended First Test

Start with **Landing Page + Campaign Support**.

Position it like this:

```text
I can build the page, set up basic tracking, and help you test it with one traffic source you already use.
If you already run ads, we can send that traffic here.
If not, we can start with Google Business Profile, SMS, or a simple local campaign before touching paid ads.
```

## Best First Channel Options

### Google Business Profile

Good first option because it is local, simple, and connected to people already searching for roofing help.

### SMS Follow-Up

Useful if Final Cut has old leads, past customers, storm inquiries, or open conversations. This can be a strong test because the traffic is warmer than cold ads.

### Facebook Or Instagram Local Post

Useful after storms or for neighborhood-specific messaging. This is easy to test, but results may vary depending on audience quality.

### Google Ads

Potentially high intent, but it increases complexity and responsibility. Use this later if Final Cut already runs ads or wants a more aggressive campaign.

## What We Track

- page visits
- form submissions
- click-to-call clicks
- CTA clicks
- traffic source
- conversion rate
- booked inspections, if Final Cut shares that result

## Who Owns What

We should own the tracking setup and performance summary.

Final Cut Roofing should own lead quality feedback, booked inspections, and closed jobs, because only they know what happens after someone calls or submits the form.

## Pilot Recommendation

For the first 30 days, keep the test simple:

- choose one traffic source
- send traffic to the focused landing page
- track form submissions and click-to-call activity
- ask Final Cut how many leads became booked inspections
- summarize what worked and what should be improved
