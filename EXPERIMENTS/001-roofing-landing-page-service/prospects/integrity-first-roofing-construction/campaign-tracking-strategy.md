# Integrity First Roofing & Construction Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Purpose

The landing page should be tested with one focused campaign at a time. The goal is to see whether a compact proof-first storm page turns more visitors into free inspection requests.

## Focused Campaign

```text
Campaign: Frisco storm damage roof inspection
Audience: Homeowners in Frisco and nearby North Texas neighborhoods
Traffic source: one selected channel
Landing page: Integrity First storm campaign page
Goal: free roof inspection requests
Tracking: visits, form submissions, click-to-call activity, and booked inspections if Integrity First shares that result
```

## Best First Channel Options

- Google Business Profile link
- Google Ads
- Facebook or Instagram local post after storms
- SMS or email follow-up to old storm inquiries
- door-hanger or mailer QR code

## What We Track

- page visits
- form submissions
- click-to-call clicks
- primary CTA clicks
- secondary CTA clicks
- traffic source
- conversion rate
- booked inspections, if Integrity First shares that result

## Who Owns What

We should own tracking setup and a short performance summary.

Integrity First should own lead quality feedback, booked inspections, and closed jobs, because only they know what happens after someone calls or submits the form.
