# Dynasty Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Campaign Goal

Test whether a short Houston post-storm inspection page converts better than sending traffic to a blog article.

## Suggested Channels

- Google Business Profile posts after storms.
- Local ads around Houston storm inspection searches.
- Follow-up links from inbound calls or estimate requests.
- Retargeting from storm-related blog content.

## Track

- Page visits.
- Phone CTA clicks.
- Free inspection form submissions.
- Source channel.
- Reply and follow-up status.

## Notes

Keep insurance language limited to documentation and guidance. Do not promise coverage.
