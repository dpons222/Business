# Dynasty Roofing Client Summary

Dynasty Roofing already has useful Houston post-storm inspection content.

The demo turns that article into a shorter inspection page built around one action: scheduling a free roof inspection after hail, wind, heavy rain, or hurricane-related roof concerns.

The page emphasizes:

- free inspections
- A+ BBB proof
- exterior and interior inspection steps
- photo documentation and transparent recommendations
