# LOA Construction Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why LOA Construction should test a focused Austin hail damage / free roof inspection landing page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is positioned as a campaign page for homeowners who recently searched after hail, wind, or severe weather.

Recommended demo direction:

```text
Light personalized version of the existing roofing landing page structure.
```

A lightweight, customer-facing demo is now prepared at:

```text
https://local-growth-preview.vercel.app/loa-construction
```

Do not expand it into a heavier custom build until LOA replies or shows interest.

## 1. Current Page Strengths

- LOA already has hail storm inspection content for Austin homeowners.
- The page gives a direct phone path: `512-375-3654`.
- The page invites homeowners to set up a free inspection after hail.
- The footer includes a request inspection / contact path.
- The business has broad Texas service-area coverage and public trust proof.

Source reviewed June 23, 2026:

```text
https://www.loaconstruction.com/blog/after-the-hail-surge-in-austin-roof-inspections-and-claims/
```

## 2. Current Page Conversion Opportunities

- The content is blog-style rather than a focused inspection request page.
- The main conversion action appears inside long educational copy.
- The page asks a storm visitor to read through many topics before taking action.
- The browser title appears mismatched with the page topic, which may reduce trust for search visitors.
- Some wording is awkward enough that a tighter campaign page could feel more direct and polished.

## 3. What The Demo Should Change

- Lead with a direct Austin hail damage inspection headline.
- Keep the phone number and request appointment path prominent.
- Turn the article into a shorter storm-response page built around one action.
- Put trust proof and reassurance near the call-to-action.
- Explain what happens next: roof check, documentation, repair/replacement options, and appointment confirmation.
- Keep insurance wording cautious and avoid promising claim outcomes.

## 4. Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your blog page is bad.
```

Say:

```text
Your Austin hail article already has the right message. I think the same idea could work harder as a short inspection page built around one action: booking a free roof inspection after hail.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
Hail Hit Austin? Book a Free Roof Inspection with LOA Construction.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Request Free Hail Inspection
```

## 5. Why LOA Is A Strong Next Prospect

LOA is a good next outreach target because the conversion improvement is easy to explain:

- They already educate homeowners about hail damage.
- They already ask homeowners to call for a free inspection.
- The existing page is long enough that a focused landing page has a clear role.
- The outreach angle can be respectful: convert useful article content into a campaign page.
- It is a lighter lift than building a brand-new visual concept from scratch.

## 6. Suggested Pilot Offer

Position the pilot as:

```text
A focused Austin hail inspection landing page that keeps LOA's free inspection message and phone path, but makes the next step easier to act on and easier to measure.
```

Suggested price test:

```text
$1,000 setup
$300/month optional tracking, edits, and testing add-on
```

Setup includes:

- one personalized landing page,
- light LOA branding and public-site details,
- inspection-focused copy,
- form and click-to-call CTAs,
- mobile-responsive layout,
- basic tracking plan,
- one revision pass after review.

Monthly add-on includes:

- form and click tracking review,
- small copy/layout improvements,
- CTA testing,
- traffic-source notes,
- monthly summary of what changed and what happened.

Conservative framing:

```text
The page is designed to make free inspection requests easier to complete and easier to measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, or booked jobs.
