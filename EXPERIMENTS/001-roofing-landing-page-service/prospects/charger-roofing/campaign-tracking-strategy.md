# Charger Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Purpose

The landing page should be tested with one focused campaign at a time. The goal is to see whether a clearer San Antonio hail damage inspection page turns more visitors into inspection requests.

## Focused Campaign

```text
Campaign: San Antonio hail damage roof inspection
Audience: Homeowners in San Antonio and nearby areas
Traffic source: one selected channel
Landing page: Charger Roofing hail inspection page
Goal: free roof inspection requests
Tracking: visits, form submissions, click-to-call activity, and booked inspections if Charger shares that result
```

The page should not be judged in isolation. It should be judged by how well it converts traffic from a specific source.

## Responsibility Levels

### Level 1: Landing Page Only

Charger Roofing chooses where to share the landing page. We build the page and set up basic tracking.

Possible uses:

- Google Business Profile link
- Facebook post
- Instagram bio or story link
- SMS follow-up link
- sales team follow-up link
- QR code on door hangers, mailers, or leave-behind material

### Level 2: Landing Page + Campaign Support

We help Charger choose one campaign channel and prepare the page link, tracking links, and basic message copy.

Possible support:

- Google Business Profile campaign link
- Facebook or Instagram post copy
- SMS follow-up message
- QR code destination
- UTM tracking links
- simple 30-day campaign review

This is the recommended starting point because it helps the client test the page properly without requiring ad management.

### Level 3: Landing Page + Ad Management

We actively manage paid traffic, such as Google Ads or Facebook/Instagram ads.

This adds responsibility for:

- ad budget recommendations
- targeting
- ad creative
- keywords
- conversion tracking
- campaign performance
- ongoing optimization

This should not be the first offer unless paid ad management becomes a deliberate service.

## Best First Channel Options

### Google Business Profile

Good first option because it is local, simple, and connected to people already searching for roofing help.

### SMS Or Email Follow-Up

Useful if Charger has old leads, past customers, storm inquiries, or open conversations. This traffic is warmer than cold ads.

### Facebook Or Instagram Local Post

Useful after storms or for neighborhood-specific messaging. This is easy to test, but results vary by audience quality.

### Google Ads

Potentially high intent, but it increases complexity and responsibility. Use later if Charger already runs ads or wants a more aggressive test.

## What We Track

- page visits
- form submissions
- click-to-call clicks
- primary CTA clicks
- secondary CTA clicks
- traffic source
- conversion rate
- booked inspections, if Charger shares that result

## Who Owns What

We should own the tracking setup and performance summary.

Charger Roofing should own lead quality feedback, booked inspections, and closed jobs, because only they know what happens after someone calls or submits the form.

## Pilot Recommendation

For the first 30 days, keep the test simple:

- choose one traffic source
- send traffic to the focused landing page
- track form submissions and click-to-call activity
- ask Charger how many leads became booked inspections
- summarize what worked and what should be improved
