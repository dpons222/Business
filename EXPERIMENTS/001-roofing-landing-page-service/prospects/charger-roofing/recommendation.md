# Charger Roofing Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why Charger Roofing should test a focused San Antonio hail damage / free roof inspection landing page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is positioned as a campaign page for homeowners who recently searched after hail, wind, or severe weather.

Recommended demo URL:

```text
/prospects/charger-roofing
```

Recommended demo direction:

```text
Storm Damage Assessment / inspection intake flow
```

## 1. Current Page Strengths

- Charger Roofing already has a dedicated hail damage page for San Antonio.
- The page already offers a free inspection / free roof checkup.
- The contact path is public and clear: phone and email are visible.
- The form is simple enough to improve around: name, email, phone, address, and message.
- The page has useful educational content about hail damage signs, hidden damage, inspection, and insurance claim considerations.
- The page has public imagery and a public HomeAdvisor badge that can inform a private demo concept.

Source reviewed June 16, 2026:

```text
https://charger-roofing.com/roof-hail-damage-in-san-antonio-texas/
```

## 2. Current Page Conversion Opportunities

- The free inspection offer is present, but it does not feel like a polished campaign page.
- The page is more educational than action-oriented.
- The request form is basic and does not have strong nearby reassurance.
- Trust proof is not prominent around the inspection call to action.
- The page could make the next step more urgent after a storm without sounding aggressive.
- The headline can be more direct for homeowners who just want to know if hail damaged their roof.

## 3. What The Demo Should Change

- Lead with a direct San Antonio hail damage headline.
- Turn the educational hail content into a practical "Do I need an inspection?" assessment.
- Let homeowners choose what happened: hail, leak, wind, or not sure.
- Let homeowners select visible damage signs before requesting the free inspection.
- Keep a clear click-to-call path beside the assessment.
- Keep the flow focused on one primary action: schedule a free hail damage inspection.
- Use simple reassurance around what happens next: inspection, visible damage review, photos/documentation, and repair or replacement options.
- Move public trust cues closer to the inspection request.
- Keep the page short enough for storm visitors who are comparing roofing companies quickly.

## 4. Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your website is bad.
```

Say:

```text
Your hail page already has the right offer. I think a more focused inspection page could make that free roof checkup easier for San Antonio homeowners to act on after a storm.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
Hail Damage in San Antonio? Schedule a Free Roof Inspection with Charger Roofing.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Schedule Free Hail Damage Inspection
```

## 5. Why Charger Is A Strong Prospect

Charger is a cleaner first outreach target than prospects with already-polished pages because the conversion improvement is easy to explain:

- They have the right offer.
- They have a dedicated topic page.
- They have contact information and a simple form.
- The visual/conversion gap is visible enough to make the demo useful.
- The outreach can be framed respectfully around making an existing offer easier to act on.

## 6. Suggested Pilot Offer

Position the pilot as:

```text
A focused San Antonio hail damage inspection landing page that keeps Charger Roofing's free inspection offer, phone number, and local positioning, but makes the request path clearer and easier to track.
```

Suggested price test:

```text
$1,000 setup
$300/month optional tracking, edits, and testing add-on
```

Setup includes:

- one personalized landing page,
- light Charger Roofing branding and public-site details,
- inspection-focused copy,
- form and click-to-call CTAs,
- mobile-responsive layout,
- basic tracking plan,
- one revision pass after review.

Monthly add-on includes:

- form and click tracking review,
- small copy/layout improvements,
- CTA testing,
- traffic-source notes,
- monthly summary of what changed and what happened.

Conservative framing:

```text
The page is designed to make free inspection requests easier to complete and easier to measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, or booked jobs.
