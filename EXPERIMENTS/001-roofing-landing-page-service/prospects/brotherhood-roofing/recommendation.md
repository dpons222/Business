# Brotherhood Roofing Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why Brotherhood Roofing should test a compact DFW hail and storm damage inspection page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is a campaign page for homeowners who recently searched after hail, wind, or severe weather.

## Current Page Strengths

- Brotherhood already has a dedicated hail and storm damage page.
- The page includes repeated free inspection CTAs.
- The page explains practical signs of residential and commercial storm damage.
- The public copy says Brotherhood serves the Dallas-Fort Worth metro area.
- The footer says Brotherhood has serviced the Metroplex since October 2002.
- Contact details are visible: `972-742-5332` and `info@brotherhoodroofing.com`.

Source reviewed June 24, 2026:

```text
https://brotherhoodroofing.com/services/hail-and-storm-damage/
```

## Conversion Opportunity

The page is helpful but long. It teaches storm damage signs, hail consequences, roof integrity issues, free inspection language, testimonials, and contact details across a long path.

A tighter campaign page could preserve the helpful education while putting the inspection request and trust proof closer together.

## Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your storm page is too long.
```

Say:

```text
Your hail and storm damage page already has useful homeowner education. I think a shorter campaign version could move more storm-concerned visitors directly to the free inspection request.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
DFW Hail or Storm Damage? Schedule a Free Roof Inspection.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Schedule Free Inspection
```

## Suggested Pilot Offer

```text
A compact DFW hail and storm damage landing page that keeps Brotherhood's free inspection offer, phone path, storm-damage signs, and Metroplex trust proof, but makes the next step easier to act on and measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, or booked jobs.
