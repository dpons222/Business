# On Point Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Campaign Goal

Test whether a shorter Plano inspection page converts better than a long free inspection article.

## Suggested Channels

- Google Business Profile posts for Plano inspection searches.
- Local ads around free roof inspection and hail searches.
- Follow-up links after inbound calls.
- QR code or SMS link after storm events.

## Track

- Page visits.
- Phone CTA clicks.
- Schedule/free inspection clicks.
- Form submissions.
- Replies and follow-up status.

## Notes

Do not guarantee same-day appointments. Use no-pressure and photo-proof language as the primary trust angle.
