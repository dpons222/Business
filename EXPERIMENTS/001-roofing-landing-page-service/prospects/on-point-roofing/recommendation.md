# On Point Roofing Recommendation

## Recommended Direction

Use a focused Plano free roof inspection landing page.

On Point already has a detailed Plano inspection page with same-day inspection proof, photo documentation, drone-ready language, reviews, and no-pressure positioning. The opportunity is to keep the personality but reduce the page into a sharper inspection request path.

## Demo Angle

```text
Plano hail or roof concerns? Schedule a free inspection or quote.
```

## Why It Fits

- The current page has useful proof but reads like a long article.
- The page already supports same-day or next-day inspections, photos, clear reports, and no-pressure recommendations.
- A shorter page could work for Plano storm searches or follow-up links.

## Guardrails

- Keep mascot/personality references light.
- Do not overpromise same-day scheduling.
- Do not overstate guarantee or review proof.
