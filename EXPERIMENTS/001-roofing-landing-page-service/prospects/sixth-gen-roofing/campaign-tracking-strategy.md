# Sixth Gen Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Campaign Goal

Test whether a focused Austin certified inspection page gets more inspection requests than a broader roofing companies page.

## Suggested Channels

- Google Business Profile posts after hail or wind.
- Local ads for Austin roof inspection searches.
- Follow-up links after inbound calls.
- QR code from local events or print material.

## Track

- Page visits.
- Phone CTA clicks.
- Schedule/free inspection clicks.
- Form submissions.
- Replies and follow-up status.

## Notes

Use HAAG-certified and evidence-based inspection language only as confirmed. Do not promise insurance results.
