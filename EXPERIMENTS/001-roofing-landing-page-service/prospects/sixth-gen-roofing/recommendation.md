# Sixth Gen Roofing Recommendation

## Recommended Direction

Use a focused Austin hail and wind inspection landing page.

Sixth Gen already has strong proof around free HAAG-certified inspections, evidence-based reports, photos, and honest recommendations. The opportunity is to put that inspection proof into a focused page for Austin homeowners after hail or wind.

## Demo Angle

```text
Austin hail or wind damage? Schedule a free certified roof inspection.
```

## Why It Fits

- The current Austin page is broad and educational.
- The inspection proof is strong enough to support a campaign page.
- The 24/7 online scheduling message gives the page a clear CTA.

## Guardrails

- Do not overstate certification claims.
- Keep insurance support language conservative.
- Do not use unverified imagery.
