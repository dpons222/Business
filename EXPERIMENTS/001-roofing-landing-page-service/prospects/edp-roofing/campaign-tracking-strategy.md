# EDP Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Purpose

The landing page should be tested with one focused campaign at a time. The goal is to see whether a simpler Dallas storm page turns more visitors into talk-to-a-roofer requests.

## Focused Campaign

```text
Campaign: Dallas storm damage roofer request
Audience: Homeowners in Dallas and nearby DFW neighborhoods
Traffic source: one selected channel
Landing page: EDP storm damage page
Goal: talk-to-a-roofer requests
Tracking: visits, form submissions, click-to-call activity, and booked inspections if EDP shares that result
```

## Best First Channel Options

- Google Business Profile link
- Google Ads
- Facebook or Instagram local post after storms
- SMS or email follow-up to old storm inquiries
- door-hanger or mailer QR code

## What We Track

- page visits
- form submissions
- click-to-call clicks
- primary CTA clicks
- secondary CTA clicks
- traffic source
- conversion rate
- booked inspections, if EDP shares that result

## Who Owns What

We should own tracking setup and a short performance summary.

EDP should own lead quality feedback, booked inspections, and closed jobs, because only they know what happens after someone calls or submits the form.
