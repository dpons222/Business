# EDP Roofing Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why EDP Roofing should test a simplified Dallas storm damage landing page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is a campaign page for homeowners who need one clear next step after hail, wind, roof leaks, or storm damage.

## Current Page Strengths

- EDP already has a dedicated Dallas storm damage roof repair page.
- The page uses a clear `Talk To A Roofer` CTA.
- The page shows 4.9 Google rating based on 22 reviews.
- It includes GAF Gold Elite, BBB A+, Google review, and other proof badges.
- It has Dallas roof project imagery and storm-damage education.
- Phone contact is visible: `(972) 274-5277`.

Source reviewed June 24, 2026:

```text
https://edproofing.com/storm-damage-roof-repair-dallas/
```

## Conversion Opportunity

The page has strong proof but many competing actions and dense service/insurance sections.

A shorter campaign page could keep the proof and storm relevance while moving visitors to one clear talk-to-a-roofer path.

## Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your page is confusing.
```

Say:

```text
Your Dallas storm page already has strong proof. I think a simplified campaign version could make the next step clearer for homeowners after hail, leaks, or wind damage.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
Dallas Storm Damage? Talk To A Roofer.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Talk To A Roofer
```

## Suggested Pilot Offer

```text
A simplified Dallas storm damage page that keeps EDP's proof badges, storm services, and phone path, but reduces competing CTAs and makes one action easier to measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, coverage approval, or booked jobs.
