# Arrington Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Campaign Goal

Test whether a short North Texas storm inspection page gets more inspection requests than sending cold or paid traffic to a long storm damage guide.

## Suggested Channels

- Google Business Profile storm-related posts.
- Local search ads after hail or wind events.
- QR code or SMS follow-up after inbound calls.
- Retargeting traffic that visited storm damage content.

## Track

- Demo page visits.
- Clicks on phone CTA.
- Inspection form submissions.
- Source channel.
- Replies to outreach.

## Notes

Do not promise lead volume or booked jobs. Treat this as a small campaign-page test.
