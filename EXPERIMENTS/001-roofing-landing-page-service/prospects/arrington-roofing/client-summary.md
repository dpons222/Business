# Arrington Roofing Client Summary

Arrington Roofing already has a detailed North Texas storm damage guide with strong homeowner education and proof.

The demo turns that broad guide into a shorter landing page focused on one action: scheduling a free storm damage roof inspection after hail, wind, tornado activity, debris, or leaks.

The page keeps the offer conservative:

- free professional inspection
- visible damage documentation
- emergency protection and repair next-step guidance
- claim-support language without outcome promises
