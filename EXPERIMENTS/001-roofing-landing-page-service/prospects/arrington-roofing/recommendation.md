# Arrington Roofing Recommendation

## Recommended Direction

Use a focused North Texas storm damage inspection landing page.

Arrington already has strong content: free inspections, extensive storm-damage education, emergency service language, documentation, and decades of North Texas experience. The opportunity is not to rewrite the full guide. It is to turn the strongest proof into one short action path for storm-concerned homeowners.

## Demo Angle

```text
North Texas storm damage? Schedule a free professional roof inspection.
```

## Why It Fits

- The current page is a comprehensive guide rather than a short campaign page.
- The page already supports the offer with free inspection, documentation, emergency service, and 42+ years of experience language.
- A shorter page could work better for Google Business Profile clicks, ads, QR codes, or storm follow-up messages.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise insurance approval, claim payout, or response time.
- Use emergency service language carefully.
- Keep the outreach framed as a focused page test, not a full website redesign.
