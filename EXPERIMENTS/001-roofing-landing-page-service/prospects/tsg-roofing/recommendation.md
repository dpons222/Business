# TSG Roofing Recommendation

## Recommendation

## Why This Prospect Fits

TSG promotes storm damage inspections, roof repairs, insurance assistance, and free consultation language across Texas metros.

## Observed Opportunity

TSG's roof repair and restoration page includes strong storm, insurance, and review content, but storm inspection is mixed with broader repair/restoration decisions.

## Demo Direction

Primary CTA:

```text
Schedule Free Roof Inspection
```

Stable URL target after deployment:

```text
https://roof-check-preview.vercel.app/tsg-roofing
```
