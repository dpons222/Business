# Proper Roofing Recommendation

## Purpose

Use this recommendation to explain why Proper Roofing should test a first-step Greater Houston storm inspection landing page.

This is not positioned as a full website replacement. It is a narrower campaign page for homeowners who need inspection and documentation before they are ready for a full restoration conversation.

## Current Page Strengths

- Proper already has a dedicated Greater Houston storm restoration page.
- The page emphasizes comprehensive inspections and full documentation.
- The public copy discusses photos, videos, storm details, repair plans, and claims assistance.
- Reviews highlight thorough explanation and professional communication.
- The page includes a free inspection / free estimate form.
- Phone contact is visible: `(832) 554-7830`.

Source reviewed June 24, 2026:

```text
https://properroof.com/storm-restoration-greater-houston/
```

## Conversion Opportunity

The page sells a complete storm restoration journey. That is strong for informed visitors, but cold homeowners may need a smaller first step first: inspection, documentation, and a clear damage review.

## Recommended Positioning

Do not say:

```text
Your restoration page is too broad.
```

Say:

```text
Your storm restoration page already has a strong documentation process. I think a narrower inspection-first campaign page could help homeowners take the first step before they are ready to discuss a full restoration project.
```

Recommended headline:

```text
Greater Houston Storm Damage? Get a Free Inspection First.
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Get My Free Inspection Today
```

## Suggested Pilot Offer

```text
A first-step storm inspection page that keeps Proper's documentation, repair-plan, and trust-over-profits message, but makes the first inspection request easier to act on and measure.
```

Avoid promising guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, coverage approval, or booked jobs.
