# Proper Roofing Campaign Tracking Strategy

## Purpose

The landing page should be tested with one focused campaign at a time. The goal is to see whether a first-step Greater Houston storm page turns more visitors into free inspection requests.

## Focused Campaign

```text
Campaign: Greater Houston storm inspection and documentation review
Audience: Homeowners in Greater Houston, Katy, Cinco Ranch, League City, and nearby areas
Traffic source: one selected channel
Landing page: Proper storm inspection page
Goal: free storm inspection requests
Tracking: visits, form submissions, click-to-call activity, and booked inspections if Proper shares that result
```

## Best First Channel Options

- Google Business Profile link
- Google Ads
- Facebook or Instagram storm-season post
- SMS or email follow-up to old storm inquiries
- door-hanger or mailer QR code

## What We Track

- page visits
- form submissions
- click-to-call clicks
- primary CTA clicks
- secondary CTA clicks
- traffic source
- conversion rate
- booked inspections, if Proper shares that result

## Who Owns What

We should own tracking setup and a short performance summary.

Proper should own lead quality feedback, booked inspections, and closed jobs, because only they know what happens after someone calls or submits the form.
