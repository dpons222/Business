# 001 Roofing Landing Page Service Plan

## Experiment Question

Will roofing contractors pay for a conversion-focused storm damage / roof inspection landing page?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Storm damage / roof inspection landing page
Target market: Roofing companies in storm-prone U.S. states
Price test: $1,000 setup
Optional add-on: $300/month for tracking, edits, and testing
Validation target: 30 prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 10 days
```

## Hypothesis

Roofing contractors with weak service pages, weak ad landing pages, or no dedicated storm damage page will be interested in a fixed-price landing page if the offer is positioned around more inspection requests, calls, and quote opportunities.

## Scope

In scope:

- Define the offer and pricing.
- Create a prospect profile.
- Research 30 roofing companies in storm-prone U.S. states.
- Create a simple outreach process.
- Track replies, objections, and interest.
- Decide whether to continue, adjust, or stop.

Out of scope for this phase:

- Building a full agency website.
- Running paid ads.
- Creating a full SaaS product.
- Building landing pages before demand is validated.
- Guaranteeing leads, rankings, or revenue.

## 10-Day Validation Plan

Day 1:

- Finalize offer, outreach message, and qualification checklist.

Days 2-3:

- Build a list of 30 qualified roofing prospects.

Days 4-6:

- Send first outreach batch.
- Track replies and objections.

Days 7-8:

- Send follow-ups.
- Book calls if prospects show interest.

Days 9-10:

- Review results.
- Decide whether to sell a paid pilot, revise the offer, test another roofing angle, or stop.

## Success Criteria

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured, or
- 3-5 positive replies are received, or
- 1-2 serious sales calls are booked with clear buying intent.

Revise if:

- Prospects respond but object to price, service angle, timing, or trust.
- Roofers prefer roof replacement, emergency leak, or general service pages instead of storm damage / inspection pages.
- Prospects want full website work instead of landing pages.

Stop or change niche if:

- 30 qualified prospects produce no replies.
- Replies show no clear pain or willingness to pay.
- Interest only appears when the work is free or heavily discounted.

## Risks

- Roofing companies may already receive many marketing pitches.
- Storm damage demand varies by state and season.
- Some companies may want lead guarantees, which should be avoided.
- The offer needs proof, examples, or a demo to feel credible.
- Claims must avoid promising leads, rankings, or insurance outcomes.
