# Personal Injury Law Growth Systems Validation

## Qualification Criteria

Qualified prospects should have:

- personal injury practice areas,
- public intake paths such as phone, chat, or forms,
- visible proof or trust surfaces,
- one public gap that can be fixed without private case data.

## Metrics

Track prospects researched, recommendation category, contact method, outreach sent, replies, objections, calls booked, paid pilots, and module interest.

## Success Criteria

Continue with 1 paid pilot, 2 serious calls, or 3-5 positive replies. Revise if firms prefer intake follow-up, proof cleanup, or specific practice-area pages over call path work. Stop if 20 qualified prospects show no credible pain.
