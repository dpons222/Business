# Discovery Call Questions

- Which case types are most important to grow?
- Where do most intake inquiries come from today?
- What happens after someone calls, chats, or submits a form?
- Are after-hours inquiries handled differently?
- Which questions help qualify a case early?
- Which proof can be used publicly with proper disclaimers?
- Which public intake path would be most useful to improve first?
- What simple signals could be tracked without private case data?
