# Marketing

Use this folder for personal injury law prospect research, outreach, validation tracking, and batch review.

## Pre-Send Checklist

- Verify firm name, location, website, and contact method.
- Re-check the intake path, practice area page, proof surface, or CTA mentioned in the draft.
- Confirm the message avoids legal advice and guaranteed outcomes.
- Confirm the recommendation is based on public evidence.
- Confirm tracker status and next follow-up date.
