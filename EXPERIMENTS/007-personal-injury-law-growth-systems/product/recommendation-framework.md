# Recommendation Framework

## Diagnostic Flow

1. Identify practice areas, intake CTAs, phone/chat/form paths, and proof surfaces.
2. Review mobile call path, practice area pages, contact page, FAQs, and disclaimers.
3. Look for visible friction that can be fixed without private case data.
4. Choose one recommendation category.
5. Keep the recommendation practical and non-legal.

## Selection Rules

Choose `consultation_call_path` when call or consultation CTAs are unclear.

Choose `car_accident_intake_page` when car accident pages are generic or weak.

Choose `case_qualification_questions` when forms exist but do not guide intake quality.

Choose `intake_followup_workflow` when response expectations are unclear.

Choose `trust_proof_cleanup` when proof exists but is scattered or risky.

## Recommendation Format

```text
Observed: [specific public issue or opportunity].
Recommendation: [one focused improvement].
Why it fits: [law-firm-specific reason].
First deliverable: [one module].
What to track: [one to three simple non-private signals].
```
