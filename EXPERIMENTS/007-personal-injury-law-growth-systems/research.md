# Personal Injury Law Growth Systems Research

## Buyer

Firm owners, managing attorneys, marketing managers, intake managers, or operations leads responsible for consumer case inquiries.

## Profit Centers

- Car accident cases.
- Truck accident cases.
- Motorcycle accident cases.
- Slip and fall cases.
- Workplace injury or construction injury cases.
- Wrongful death inquiries.
- Medical malpractice if the firm handles it.
- Faster intake and follow-up for high-intent leads.

## Common Visible Gaps

| Visible gap | Customer action affected | Business value | Possible module | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consultation CTA is unclear | Calls and forms | More intake opportunities | Free consultation call path cleanup | Header, mobile CTA, contact page |
| Practice area page is generic | Case-specific inquiries | Clearer fit for high-intent visitors | Car accident intake page | Practice area pages, FAQs |
| Case qualification is unclear | Intake quality | Better lead routing | Case qualification question set | Forms, consultation copy |
| Follow-up expectations missing | Signed client potential | Faster response consistency | Intake follow-up workflow | Forms, thank-you pages, chat widgets |
| Proof is hard to evaluate | Trust | Higher confidence | Trust proof cleanup | Results, reviews, testimonials, disclaimers |

## Initial Wedge

Start with consultation call path and car accident intake clarity. These are easy to inspect publicly and close to intake value.
