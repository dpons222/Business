# 007 Personal Injury Law Growth Systems

Validation experiment for AI-assisted growth recommendations and focused implementation modules for personal injury law firms.

## Core Test

```text
Will personal injury law firms pay for a practical, personalized recommendation and implementation package based on the biggest visible gap in their intake, practice-area, trust, call, or follow-up journey?
```

## Current Offer

```text
Primary offer: Personal injury firm growth audit + one focused implementation module
Target market: Personal injury firms with visible intake, case type, trust proof, call path, or follow-up gaps
Price test: $1,500 setup for one focused module
Optional add-on: $400/month for tracking review, page refreshes, intake workflow maintenance, or content support
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Solution Modules

- Free consultation call path cleanup.
- Car accident intake page.
- Slip and fall or workplace injury page.
- Case qualification question set.
- Intake follow-up workflow.
- Trust proof and settlement language cleanup.
- Review and testimonial workflow.
- Local service area/practice area cleanup.
- FAQ and expectation-setting module.
- Intake status tracker and scripts.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise cases, signed clients, settlements, verdicts, rankings, reviews, revenue, or ad performance.
- Do not provide legal advice.
- Do not invent case results, credentials, awards, settlement amounts, or client testimonials.
- Keep outreach and recommendations conservative and evidence-based.
