# Personal Injury Law Growth Systems Plan

## Experiment Question

Will personal injury law firms pay for one focused growth improvement selected from their visible intake, practice-area, trust, call, or follow-up journey?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Personal injury intake journey audit + one focused implementation module
First modules to test: consultation call path, car accident intake page, intake follow-up workflow, proof and FAQ cleanup
Target market: Personal injury firms with consumer-facing case intake
Price test: $1,500 setup
Optional add-on: $400/month
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

Personal injury firms operate in a high-value, competitive market where intake clarity, trust, urgency, and follow-up matter. A focused public journey improvement can be easier to test than a broad SEO or ad engagement.

- Full SEO campaigns.
- Paid ads.
- Case result guarantees.
