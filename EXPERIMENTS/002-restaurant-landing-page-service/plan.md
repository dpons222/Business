# 002 Restaurant Landing Page Service Plan

## Experiment Question

Will local restaurants pay for a focused landing page that makes one high-value customer action easier to complete?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Private dining, catering, event, or seasonal campaign landing page
Target market: Independent local restaurants with visible demand signals and incomplete conversion paths
Price test: $750 setup
Optional add-on: $250/month for edits, tracking review, and campaign support
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

Independent restaurants often have strong food, brand, and local trust signals, but their websites spread important actions across menu PDFs, social links, reservation widgets, and generic contact pages. A focused campaign page can help owners test one specific action, such as catering inquiries or private dining requests, without rebuilding the whole site.

## Recommended Wedge

Start with private dining, catering, and event inquiry pages.

This is stronger than a generic restaurant homepage redesign because group meals, catering, parties, and events are usually higher-value opportunities than one-off diners. The page can be positioned as a focused campaign link for Google Business Profile, Instagram, email, QR cards, or existing site traffic.

## Scope

In scope:

- Define the restaurant-specific offer and price test.
- Create prospect qualification criteria.
- Create restaurant outreach and follow-up scripts.
- Create discovery call questions.
- Create a reusable demo page spec and content model.
- Create prospect tracking files.
- Prepare the experiment for a future first outreach batch.

Out of scope for this phase:

- Building demo app code.
- Researching real prospects.
- Running ads.
- Managing reservations, delivery, point-of-sale, or ordering systems.
- Replacing a restaurant's full website.
- Guaranteeing reservations, orders, leads, revenue, rankings, or campaign outcomes.

## 14-Day Validation Plan

Days 1-2:

- Finalize offer, demo spec, outreach message, and qualification criteria.

Days 3-5:

- Build a list of 20 qualified restaurant prospects in one chosen market.
- Prioritize businesses with a clear catering, events, private dining, seasonal, or takeout angle.

Days 6-8:

- Create 3-5 light personalized demo directions.
- Prepare exact outreach drafts and run the pre-send checklist.

Days 9-11:

- Send first outreach batch after review.
- Track replies, objections, and calls.

Days 12-14:

- Send follow-ups.
- Decide whether to sell a paid pilot, adjust the offer, change the restaurant segment, or stop.

## Success Criteria

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured, or
- 2 serious sales calls are booked, or
- 3-5 positive replies indicate interest in a focused restaurant campaign page.

Revise if:

- Restaurants like the concept but object to the price, offer angle, timing, or platform fit.
- Owners prefer a different wedge, such as takeout ordering, brunch reservations, holiday dining, or gift cards.
- Prospects only want full website redesigns instead of focused campaign pages.

Stop or change niche if:

- 20 qualified prospects produce no replies.
- Replies show no clear pain or willingness to pay.
- Interest only appears when the work is free or heavily discounted.

## Risks

- Many restaurant owners are busy and may not respond to generic marketing pitches.
- Margins can be thin, so the offer must connect to a practical business action.
- Some restaurants rely on third-party platforms and may not want separate pages.
- Website copy must avoid overclaiming outcomes or implying operational capabilities that the restaurant does not offer.

## Next Action

Research a first batch of 20 qualified restaurants in one market, then pick 3-5 for light personalized demo directions.
