## FAQ Starters

```text
Can you handle large groups?
```

Use only if the restaurant has a clear group process.

```text
How much notice do catering orders need?
```

Use only when the restaurant can provide a real lead-time rule.

```text
Can guests choose from the regular menu?
```

Use only when menu rules are confirmed.

```text
Where are you located?
```

Use for any page where guests may arrive from social, search, email, or QR traffic.
