# Base Wireframe

## Page Structure

### 1. Header

Content:

- Restaurant name or logo.
- Short offer label.
- Primary CTA button.
- Phone or secondary action.

Goal:

Make the action obvious immediately on mobile and desktop.

### 2. Hero

Content:

- Guest-facing headline.
- One short support line.
- Primary CTA.
- One trust or context signal.
- Optional hero image if approved or publicly usable.

Goal:

Clarify what the guest can do and why this page exists.

### 3. Menu / Package Highlights

Content:

- 3-6 menu highlights, package options, or experience highlights.
- Short descriptions.
- Optional pricing only if public and current.

Goal:

Help guests understand fit quickly without reading a full menu.

### 4. Occasion Fit

Content:

- Office lunches.
- Birthdays.
- Team dinners.
- Rehearsal dinners.
- Holiday meals.
- Patio events.
- Family gatherings.

Goal:

Show the guest when this offer makes sense.

### 5. How It Works

Content:

- Step 1: choose the date or offer.
- Step 2: send details or reserve.
- Step 3: restaurant confirms availability or order details.

Goal:

Reduce hesitation and prevent avoidable questions.

### 6. Trust / Local Proof

Content:

- Years open, neighborhood, review count, awards, press, chef note, local sourcing, or other public proof.

Goal:

Use real proof without fabricating claims.

### 7. Location / Hours / Contact

Content:

- Address.
- Neighborhood.
- Hours summary.
- Phone.
- Parking or pickup details if public.

Goal:

Answer practical questions before the CTA.

### 8. FAQ

Content:

- 3-5 practical questions tied to the selected action.

Goal:

Handle common booking, ordering, or inquiry concerns.

### 9. Final CTA

Content:

- Restated action.
- Primary CTA.
- Secondary call option if useful.

Goal:

Give guests one final clear path.
