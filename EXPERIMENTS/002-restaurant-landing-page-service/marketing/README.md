# Marketing

Use this folder for restaurant prospect research, outreach, validation tracking, and batch review.

## Files

- `prospect-tracker.csv`: active prospect tracker.
- `prospect-tracker.md`: tracker field guide and status values.
- `outreach-script.md`: first message, follow-ups, and channel guidance.
- `discovery-call-questions.md`: questions for interested restaurant owners or managers.

## Rule

Keep outreach specific, respectful, and focused on one restaurant action: catering, private dining, events, group bookings, seasonal reservations, gift cards, or online ordering.

Do not claim guaranteed reservations, orders, catering leads, event bookings, revenue, rankings, or ad performance.

## Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending any outreach email, contact form message, or DM:

- Re-open the prospect draft and read the exact message that will be sent.
- Verify the restaurant name, contact method, location, website, and offer angle.
- Verify the draft matches the restaurant's current website, menu, brand colors, and public information.
- If a demo link is included, open it and confirm it loads the current prospect page.
- Confirm the demo uses the stable production URL when available, not a temporary immutable deployment URL.
- Confirm the CTA is appropriate for the restaurant, such as catering inquiry, private dining request, reserve, order, call, or email.
- Confirm the tracker status and next follow-up date are ready to update after sending.

## Batch Approval Workflow

Use this when preparing 3-5 restaurants for an outreach batch:

1. Research each restaurant and document one specific opportunity.
2. Prepare the exact outreach draft.
3. Create or refresh a light demo only when it can be done quickly.
4. Complete the pre-send checklist for each prospect.
5. Mark the row `ready_for_review`.
6. Diego reviews the draft, demo link, contact method, and fit.
7. Send only after approval.
8. Update the tracker with send date, status, reply, and follow-up date.

Do not mark a prospect as ready for review just because the business exists. Ready for review means the exact message, contact method, offer angle, and any demo link have been checked.
