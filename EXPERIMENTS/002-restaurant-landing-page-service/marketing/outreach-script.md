# Outreach Script

## First Message: Catering / Private Dining

Subject:

```text
Quick page idea for [Restaurant Name]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Restaurant Name] and noticed [specific public detail: catering, private dining, group meals, patio events, brunch, holiday meals, etc.].

One idea: a focused page for [target action] that gives guests one clear path to [request catering / ask about private dining / book a group meal / reserve for the seasonal event], instead of making them piece it together from the menu, contact page, and social links.

I am testing this as a fixed-scope service for local restaurants. The page would use your existing menu, brand, photos, and links, and would be built around one measurable action.

Would it be useful if I sent over a quick example direction for [Restaurant Name]?

Diego
```

## First Message: Seasonal Campaign

Subject:

```text
Idea for your [season/event] page
```

Body:

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed [Restaurant Name] has a strong fit for [holiday / brunch / patio season / tasting menu / game day / local event].

I am testing focused restaurant landing pages for campaigns like this: one page with the offer, menu highlights, date or availability details, location, FAQs, and a clear [reserve / call / order / inquire] button.

The goal is not to replace your website. It is to give one campaign a cleaner link for Google, Instagram, email, QR cards, or your current site.

Would you be open to seeing a quick example direction for [Restaurant Name]?

Diego
```

## Follow-Up 1

Send 3-5 business days after the first message.

```text
Hi [Name],

Quick follow-up on the page idea for [target action].

The reason I thought it might fit [Restaurant Name] is that [specific observed issue or opportunity]. A focused page could make the next step clearer for guests without changing your whole site.

Worth a quick look, or should I leave it?

Diego
```

## Follow-Up 2

Send 5-7 business days after follow-up 1.

```text
Hi [Name],

Last note from me.

If [target action] becomes a priority later, I think [Restaurant Name] could test it with one focused page and simple tracking before committing to anything bigger.

Thanks for taking a look.

Diego
```

## Contact Form Version

Use this when the contact form is short.

```text
Hi, I had a focused page idea for [Restaurant Name]'s [catering/private dining/events/seasonal reservations] offer. The page would use your existing menu, brand, and links to make one action easier: [request catering / ask about private dining / reserve / order]. I am testing this as a fixed-scope service for local restaurants. If useful, I can send a quick example direction. - Diego
```

## Guardrails

- Mention one real observed opportunity.
- Keep the message short.
- Do not imply the restaurant has a broken website.
- Do not promise reservations, orders, catering leads, revenue, rankings, or ad performance.
- Do not send a demo link until the page is reviewed and the pre-send checklist is complete.
