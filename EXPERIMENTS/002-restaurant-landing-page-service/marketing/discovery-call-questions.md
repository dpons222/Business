# Discovery Call Questions

Use these when a restaurant owner, manager, catering contact, or events contact shows interest.

## Fit

- Which action matters most right now: catering, private dining, group bookings, seasonal reservations, takeout, gift cards, or something else?
- What makes that action valuable for the restaurant?
- How do guests currently find out about it?
- Where do you currently send people from Google, Instagram, email, QR cards, or ads?

## Current Flow

- What page or platform handles this today?
- What questions do guests usually ask before they book, order, or inquire?
- Where do people get stuck or call with confusion?
- Which link should the page use for the primary CTA?

## Content

- Which menu items, packages, occasions, or photos should be highlighted?
- Do you have approved photos we can use?
- Are there brand colors, fonts, or logos that should guide the page?
- Are there private dining, catering, group booking, or event policies we need to state clearly?

## Operations

- Who receives inquiries?
- What details do you need from a guest before you can respond?
- How quickly can your team respond to inquiries?
- Are there blackout dates, minimums, capacity limits, or lead-time requirements?

## Tracking

- What would make this pilot worth continuing?
- Do you care most about calls, inquiry forms, reservation clicks, order clicks, or email requests?
- Do you already track website clicks, calls, reservations, or orders?
- How often would you want a short performance review?

## Close

- If I build the first page, who needs to approve it?
- What would make you comfortable starting with a fixed-scope pilot?
- Are there any platforms, links, or brand rules I should know before proposing the page?
