# Validation

## Validation Target

Contact 20 qualified independent restaurants in one chosen market and test whether the focused campaign page offer produces meaningful interest.

## Qualified Prospect Criteria

A qualified prospect should have most of these:

- Independent or small local restaurant group.
- Public website or active Google Business Profile.
- Public menu, hours, phone, and address.
- Clear visual assets, brand colors, food photos, or social proof.
- A specific high-value action to promote, such as catering, events, private dining, seasonal reservations, takeout, or gift cards.
- A current website path where that action is unclear, buried, slow, generic, or split across tools.
- A likely reachable owner, manager, catering contact, or events contact.

## Disqualifiers

- No visible contact path.
- No clear offer beyond normal walk-in dining.
- Corporate chain with centralized marketing.
- Existing campaign pages are already strong and specific.
- The owner expects guaranteed reservations, orders, revenue, search rankings, or ad results.

## Metrics

Track:

- Qualified prospects found.
- Outreach messages sent.
- Positive replies.
- Neutral replies.
- Objections.
- Calls booked.
- Paid pilot commitments.
- Demo page visits, if a demo is sent with tracking.
- CTA clicks or inquiry form submissions, if a pilot is launched.

## Decision Rules

Continue if:

- 1 paid pilot is secured.
- 2 serious sales calls are booked.
- 3-5 positive replies show the offer is worth another batch.

Revise if:

- Restaurants like the idea but want a different wedge.
- Prospects object to price more than concept.
- The best prospects respond better to a lower-risk pilot, such as a seasonal campaign page.

Archive or change niche if:

- 20 qualified prospects produce no replies.
- Replies show no urgent problem or willingness to pay.
- The service becomes too custom before there is payment.

## Evidence To Capture

For each outreach batch:

- Prospect list and qualification notes.
- Exact outreach drafts.
- Demo links, if any.
- Pre-send checklist evidence.
- Sent dates and follow-up dates.
- Reply summaries and objections.
- Decision after the batch.
