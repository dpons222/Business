# Finances

## Price Test

```text
Setup price: $750
Optional monthly add-on: $250/month
```

## Setup Scope Assumption

The setup price assumes:

- One focused landing page.
- Existing restaurant assets and public information are enough for a first version.
- One primary CTA.
- One revision round.
- No complex integrations beyond linking to existing reservation, ordering, phone, email, or form tools.

## Monthly Scope Assumption

The optional monthly add-on covers:

- Minor copy and content edits.
- Seasonal campaign updates.
- Link and CTA checks.
- Basic tracking review.
- A short performance summary.
- One recommended next test per month.

## Expected Costs

Likely direct costs per unpaid validation batch:

- Domain: $0 if using existing preview domain.
- Hosting: $0 incremental if using the existing Vercel setup or static docs only.
- Email: $0 incremental for manual outreach or approved existing automation.
- Research time: primary cost.
- Demo personalization time: keep under 5-10 minutes before a positive reply.

Likely direct costs per paid pilot:

- Hosting: low or covered by existing infrastructure.
- Analytics: low or included in current tools.
- Email/form routing: low if using existing tools.
- Stock or paid images: avoid unless the client approves.

## Break-Even Notes

At $750 setup:

- 1 paid pilot validates willingness to pay and funds more testing.
- 2 paid pilots can justify building a reusable coded demo template.
- Monthly add-ons become meaningful only if clients value edits, tracking checks, and seasonal campaign support.

## Assumptions

- Restaurants are more likely to pay when the page supports a specific business action.
- High-value actions such as catering, private dining, and events are more compelling than generic homepage improvements.
- A light personalized demo can improve reply quality, but unpaid customization must remain limited.

## Financial Guardrails

- Do not discount below the point where setup time becomes unsustainable.
- Do not include custom integrations in the base setup price.
- Do not absorb paid photo, ad, reservation, or ordering platform costs unless the client approves them.
- Do not sell ongoing reporting unless tracking exists and the client understands what is being reviewed.
