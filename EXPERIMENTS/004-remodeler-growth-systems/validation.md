# Remodeler Growth Systems Validation

## Qualification Criteria

Qualified prospects should have:

- residential remodeling services,
- active website,
- visible project proof or service pages,
- a clear consultation or estimate path,
- one visible gap that can be fixed with a focused module.

## Metrics

Track prospects researched, recommendation category, outreach status, replies, objections, calls booked, paid pilots, and most requested modules.

## Success Criteria

Continue with 1 paid pilot, 2 serious calls, or 3-5 positive replies. Revise if owners prefer a different wedge such as financing, portfolio cleanup, or follow-up. Stop if 20 qualified prospects show no credible pain.
