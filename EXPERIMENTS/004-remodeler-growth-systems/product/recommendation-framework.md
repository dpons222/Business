# Recommendation Framework

## Diagnostic Flow

1. Identify main project types and service area.
2. Review homepage, service pages, portfolio, consultation CTAs, and financing/budget language.
3. Look for visible friction or weak proof.
4. Choose one recommendation category.
5. Create a light recommendation only if the evidence supports it.

## Selection Rules

Choose `consultation_request_flow` when the next step is unclear.

Choose `portfolio_proof_cleanup` when strong project proof exists but is hard to scan.

Choose `financing_budget_guidance` when budget concerns likely block inquiries.

Choose `estimate_followup_workflow` when forms exist but follow-up expectations are unclear.

## Recommendation Format

```text
Observed: [specific public issue or opportunity].
Recommendation: [one focused improvement].
Why it fits: [remodeler-specific reason].
First deliverable: [one module].
What to track: [one to three simple signals].
```
