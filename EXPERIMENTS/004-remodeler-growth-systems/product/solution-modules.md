# Solution Modules

## 1. Consultation Request Flow Cleanup

Clarifies the next step for high-intent homeowners and reduces friction around requesting a consultation.

## 2. Kitchen Or Bath Campaign Page

Creates a focused path for a high-value project type with proof, FAQs, CTA, and tracking plan.

## 3. Portfolio And Proof Organization

Turns scattered photos, reviews, and project details into a clearer trust-building path.

## 4. Financing And Budget Guidance Module

Explains budget ranges, financing prompts, and expectation-setting without inventing pricing.

## 5. Estimate Follow-Up Workflow

Defines status values, email follow-ups, and next steps after inquiries.

## 6. Design-Build Process Explanation

Clarifies process stages, consultation expectations, and what homeowners need to prepare.

## 7. Project Type Landing Page

Builds a narrow page for basements, additions, outdoor living, or whole-home remodels.

## 8. Review And Case Study Capture Workflow

Creates an approved process for gathering, organizing, and reusing project proof.
