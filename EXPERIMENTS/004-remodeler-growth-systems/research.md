# Remodeler Growth Systems Research

## Buyer

Owners, general managers, marketing managers, or office admins at residential remodeling companies.

## Profit Centers

- Kitchen remodel consultations.
- Bathroom remodel consultations.
- Basement finishing projects.
- Design-build projects.
- Whole-home remodels.
- Additions and outdoor living projects.
- Financing-assisted projects.

## Common Visible Gaps

| Visible gap | Customer action affected | Business value | Possible module | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consultation CTA is buried | Consultation requests | More qualified sales conversations | Consultation request flow cleanup | Homepage, project pages, mobile header |
| Portfolio is hard to scan | Trust and comparison | Better proof for high-ticket projects | Portfolio and proof organization | Gallery, case studies, project details |
| Budget/financing guidance is missing | Buyer confidence | Reduces hesitation | Financing and budget guidance module | Financing page, FAQ, pricing language |
| Project type pages are generic | Search and conversion | Clearer fit for high-intent visitors | Kitchen/bath campaign page | Service pages, title copy, CTAs |
| Follow-up process is invisible | Estimate progression | Better pipeline consistency | Estimate follow-up workflow | Forms, thank-you copy, inquiry flow |

## Initial Wedge

Start with consultation request flow and portfolio proof cleanup because both are visible, close to revenue, and easy to explain.
