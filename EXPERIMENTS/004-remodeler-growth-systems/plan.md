# Remodeler Growth Systems Plan

## Experiment Question

Will remodelers pay for one focused growth improvement selected from their visible consultation, portfolio, quote, or follow-up journey?

## Decision

```text
Primary offer angle: Remodeler customer journey audit + one focused implementation module
First modules to test: consultation request flow, portfolio proof cleanup, kitchen/bath campaign page, estimate follow-up workflow
Target market: Residential remodelers and design-build firms
Price test: $1,250 setup
Optional add-on: $350/month
Validation target: 20 qualified prospects
Success signal: 1 paid pilot, 2 serious sales calls, or 3-5 positive replies
Timebox: 14 days
```

## Hypothesis

Remodelers lose high-intent prospects when project pages, galleries, budgets, financing, proof, and consultation CTAs are unclear. A focused module can help owners test a cleaner path to qualified conversations without rebuilding the whole site.
