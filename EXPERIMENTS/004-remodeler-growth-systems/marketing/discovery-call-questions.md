# Discovery Call Questions

- Which project types are most valuable right now?
- Where do consultation requests usually come from?
- What makes a lead qualified enough for a sales conversation?
- What happens after someone requests an estimate?
- Are project photos, case studies, or testimonials easy to publish?
- Do budget, financing, or timeline questions block prospects before a call?
- Which single public journey would be most useful to improve first?
- What simple signals could you track during a pilot?
