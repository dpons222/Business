# Scoring Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire before scoring business ideas. The default answers below are practical starting assumptions for a remote, AI-assisted business lab. Edit any answer that does not match your actual preferences.

## Current Priorities

```text
How much time can you realistically spend per week?
Default answer: 10-15 focused hours per week.

How quickly do you want the first validation test completed?
Default answer: Within 1-2 weeks.

How quickly do you want a realistic chance at first revenue?
Default answer: Within 30-60 days.

What startup budget are you comfortable risking?
Default answer: $0-$300 per idea before validation.

Are you willing to spend money on ads, tools, domains, or subscriptions before revenue?
Default answer: Yes, but only small amounts for clear validation tests or necessary tools.

Do you prefer selling products, services, software, content, ecommerce, or a mix?
Default answer: A mix, with preference for digital products, productized services, simple apps, and automations.

Do you want something mostly solo, or are you open to hiring contractors later?
Default answer: Mostly solo at first, open to contractors later if revenue justifies it.

Do you prefer building once and selling repeatedly, or doing client/service work first?
Default answer: Prefer scalable products long term, but willing to use services first to get faster revenue and market feedback.

How comfortable are you with direct outreach or sales calls?
Default answer: Somewhat comfortable with direct outreach; prefer async messages and simple calls over heavy sales.

How comfortable are you with creating public content on social media?
Default answer: Somewhat comfortable, especially if content can be systemized or partially faceless.

How comfortable are you with showing your face/name publicly?
Default answer: Prefer optional or limited personal visibility; faceless or brand-led approaches are acceptable.

Which platforms do you already know or want to use? Examples: YouTube, TikTok, Etsy, Shopify, Gumroad, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, SEO.
Default answer: Instagram, SEO, Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Etsy, and simple landing pages.

Which platforms do you want to avoid?
Default answer: Avoid platforms that require constant live engagement, heavy inventory management, or high upfront ad spend.

Which industries, hobbies, or customer types interest you?
Default answer: Remote workers, freelancers, small businesses, creators, ecommerce sellers, local service businesses, and AI-curious professionals.

Which industries, hobbies, or customer types do you want to avoid?
Default answer: Avoid heavily regulated niches such as medical, legal, investment advice, insurance, and complex tax work unless handled carefully.

How important is passive or semi-passive income compared with faster active income?
Default answer: Important long term, but faster active income is acceptable for validation and learning.

How important is travel flexibility?
Default answer: Very important.

How important is low customer support burden?
Default answer: Important; avoid ideas that require constant urgent support.

How important is avoiding legal, tax, medical, financial, or compliance-heavy niches?
Default answer: Very important.

What skills do you already have that could create an advantage?
Default answer: Ability to use Codex/AI, organize projects, build or coordinate digital assets, research markets, and create structured systems.

What do you want Codex/AI to help with most? Examples: websites, apps, automations, content, research, products, operations.
Default answer: Websites, apps, automations, research, content systems, digital products, validation assets, and operating playbooks.

What would make a business idea feel exciting enough to keep working on?
Default answer: Clear path to remote income, low upfront cost, useful product or service, strong AI leverage, and realistic first validation within 1-2 weeks.

What would make a business idea an automatic no?
Default answer: High upfront cost, in-person dependency, heavy regulation, unclear monetization, constant support burden, or needing months of work before validation.
```

## Scoring Weights

Assign each category a weight from 1 to 3.

Use:

- `1` for useful but not decisive
- `2` for important
- `3` for critical

Default weights:

```text
Customer pain or desire: 3
Ability to reach customers: 3
Monetization clarity: 3
Speed to validate: 3
Low startup cost: 3
AI/Codex leverage: 3
Remote suitability: 3
Operational simplicity: 2
Defensibility or differentiation: 2
Personal interest: 2
```

## Weighted Score Method

Each idea gets a raw score from 1 to 5 in every category.

```text
Weighted category score = raw score x category weight
Total weighted score = sum of all weighted category scores
```

This creates rankings that reflect your priorities. For example, if travel flexibility and low startup cost are critical, ideas that are remote-friendly and cheap to test will rise above ideas that might be profitable but operationally heavy.

## Score Interpretation

With the default weights, the maximum weighted score is 135.

```text
110-135: Strong candidate for deeper research
90-109: Possible candidate, compare against stronger ideas
70-89: Weak unless there is a special advantage
Below 70: Archive or revisit later
```
