# Ideas Backlog

Use this file for raw business ideas before they become researched concepts or experiments.

## Intake Rules

- Capture ideas quickly.
- Do not overbuild the idea at intake.
- Add enough detail to make the next review useful.
- Score only after there is enough context.
- Move weak ideas to `archived.md`.
- Move strong ideas into deeper research before creating an experiment folder.

## Raw Ideas

Scores use the default weighted scoring model in `IDEAS/scoring-questionnaire.md`. Maximum score: 135.
