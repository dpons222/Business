# Business

Remote business lab for researching, validating, and building AI-assisted business ideas.

## Directory Guide

```text
IDEAS/
```

Raw business ideas, idea templates, scoring notes, and archived concepts before they become experiments.

```text
RESEARCH/
```

Market, niche, competitor, pricing, channel, and trend research that may support multiple ideas or experiments.

```text
EXPERIMENTS/
```

Active or completed validation tests. Each experiment should be self-contained with its own plan, offer, research, validation notes, finances, product files, marketing files, and assets.

```text
ASSETS/
```

Reusable assets shared across ideas or experiments, such as brand materials, prompt libraries, templates, reusable media, and social formats.

```text
SKILLS/
```

Reusable Codex/AI instructions, specialized workflows, or project-specific operating skills.

```text
AUTOMATIONS/
```

Automation plans, workflow specs, n8n/Zapier notes, scripts, and operational automation documents.

```text
PLAYBOOKS/
```

Repeatable processes and checklists for validation, launches, ecommerce, content, marketing, and operations.
