# Validation Playbook

Use this playbook before building a full product, service, app, website, or content system.

## Goal

Validate whether a real customer segment has enough pain, desire, or willingness to pay before investing heavily.

## Process

1. Define the customer.
2. Define the painful problem or desired outcome.
3. Identify existing alternatives.
4. Write the smallest credible offer.
5. Choose one validation test.
6. Run the test for a limited period.
7. Record evidence.
8. Decide whether to continue, change, or archive.

## Validation Tests

- Landing page with email capture
- Direct outreach to a defined customer segment
- Marketplace listing
- Social content test
- Paid ad smoke test with a small budget
- Preorder, waitlist, or deposit
- Manual service delivery before automation
- Prototype shared with a niche community
- Competitor pricing and demand analysis

## Decision Guide

- Continue if there is clear demand, reachable customers, and a believable path to revenue.
- Change the idea if the customer or offer is wrong but the problem appears real.
- Archive if there is weak demand, unclear monetization, or too much operational risk.
