# Brand

Public-facing positioning and credibility material for the business.

## Purpose

Use this folder to define how the business presents itself before it has a large portfolio.

The brand should feel:

- specific enough to be credible,
- broad enough to support future verticals,
- conservative about outcomes,
- practical and service-oriented,
- focused on helping local service businesses turn existing offers into clearer campaign pages.

## Current Positioning Draft

```text
DigiDap builds focused landing pages, tracking systems, and digital assets for local service businesses.

Current focus: roofing companies that want a clearer post-storm inspection request path.
```

## Name Direction

```text
DigiDap
```

Rationale:

- "Digi" signals digital assets and web systems.
- "Dap" connects to the founder initials, D A P.
- The name can support websites, automations, AI images, and practical digital assets without locking the business into one service.

Current tone target:

```text
Modern utility, practical, direct, local-business friendly.
```

## Credibility Principles

- Use real demos and clear explanations instead of inflated claims.
- Do not imply a prospect approved a demo unless they have.
- Say "demo concept" for unpaid personalized examples.
- Avoid guaranteed leads, rankings, revenue, claim outcomes, or booked jobs.
- Keep the first public site simple until there is proof from outreach.

## Open Decisions

- Business name.
- Domain name.
- Email domain.
- Whether the first public site is personal-brand-first or niche-offer-first.
