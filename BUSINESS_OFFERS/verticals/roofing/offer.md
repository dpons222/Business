# Roofing Offer

## Offer

```text
A focused storm-response landing page that helps homeowners request a free roof inspection after hail, wind, or severe weather.
```

## What It Includes

- Personalized page copy using public business details.
- Clear storm/hail inspection headline.
- Click-to-call CTA.
- Short inspection request form.
- Trust/reassurance sections near the form.
- Mobile-responsive layout.
- Basic tracking plan for form submissions, CTA clicks, and click-to-call actions.

## Best-Fit Prospects

- Roofing companies with a free inspection offer.
- Companies with storm, hail, or wind damage pages that feel broad or educational.
- Companies already running ads, using Google Business Profile, or sending follow-up links after storms.

## Not A Fit

- Companies with no clear inspection offer.
- Companies that need a full rebrand or full website rebuild first.
- Companies expecting guaranteed leads, rankings, claim outcomes, or revenue.

## Current Pilot Price

```text
$1,000 setup
$300/month optional tracking, edits, and testing
```
