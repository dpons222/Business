# Roofing Outreach Positioning

## Core Angle

```text
Your site already has the right offer. I made a focused page concept that makes the free inspection easier to request after a storm.
```

## First Message Shape

```text
Hi [Company],

I noticed your [city/service] page already offers [free inspection/free checkup].

I put together a focused landing page concept that keeps your positioning and contact path, but makes the inspection request easier to act on after [hail/wind/storm event].

Demo: [link]

The idea is not to replace your full website. It would be a campaign page you can test from Google Business Profile clicks, local ads, QR codes, or follow-up messages.

If useful, I can help set it up and track form submissions, click-to-call activity, and CTA clicks so you can compare it against the current path.
```

## Strong Phrases

- "focused landing page concept"
- "campaign page"
- "free inspection request path"
- "easier to act on"
- "easier to track"
- "short pilot"

## Phrases To Avoid

- "Your website is bad."
- "Guaranteed more leads."
- "Rank higher."
- "Fix your website."
- "AI-powered everything."
