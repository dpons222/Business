# Pricing Patterns

## Current Test Pricing

For the roofing landing page offer:

```text
$1,000 setup
$300/month optional tracking, edits, and testing
```

## Positioning

Setup includes:

- one focused landing page,
- light public-brand personalization,
- mobile-responsive layout,
- form and click-to-call CTAs,
- basic tracking plan,
- one revision pass.

Optional monthly support includes:

- form submission tracking,
- click-to-call tracking,
- small copy/layout improvements,
- CTA testing,
- monthly performance summary.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed leads.
- Do not promise rankings or ad performance.
- Do not promise claim outcomes, booked jobs, or revenue.
- Phrase the value as making an offer easier to act on and easier to measure.
