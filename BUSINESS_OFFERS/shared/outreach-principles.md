# Outreach Principles

## Core Approach

- Keep outreach specific and respectful.
- Mention one observed opportunity.
- Show one relevant demo or example.
- Ask for a low-pressure next step.
- Track every contact and follow-up.

## Messaging Pattern

```text
I noticed [specific public page/offer].
You already have [strength].
I made a focused page concept that helps [one action].
Here is the demo: [link].
If useful, I can help set it up and track [measurable events].
```

## Avoid

- "Your website is bad."
- "I guarantee more leads."
- "This will rank better."
- "This will increase revenue."
- Long explanations of what the business already does.
- Sending every internal variant or dashboard link.
