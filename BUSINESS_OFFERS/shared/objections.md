# Objection Notes

## "We already have a website."

Response angle:

```text
This is not meant to replace the full website. It is a focused campaign page for one offer and one traffic path.
```

## "How do we know it works?"

Response angle:

```text
Treat it as a short pilot. Track form submissions, click-to-call actions, and CTA clicks so the page can be compared against the current path.
```

## "We do not want another complicated tool."

Response angle:

```text
The page can be simple: one offer, one phone CTA, one form, and basic tracking.
```

## "Can you guarantee leads?"

Response angle:

```text
No. The goal is to make the offer clearer to act on and easier to measure. Traffic quality, market timing, and follow-up still matter.
```
